# Production Team, Costs and Process: Self-Published 32-Page Picture Book (alt: Chapter Book / Illustrated Collection), Canada, 2026

Conventions: USD amounts are converted at 1 USD = 1.4369 CAD, the Bank of Canada rate for 2026-11-23 — [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/). I did the conversions and rounded them. "Unverified" means I saw it only in a search snippet or the page would not open. [PRE-2023] flags cost data older than 2023. Source dates appear in the link labels. "$" without a code means the source did not name a currency.

## 1. Illustration (cost, cover, royalties, contracts, sourcing, process, files, ownership, Canadian references)

### Takeaway
On curated marketplaces, a 32-page picture book costs about USD 2,000–10,000 (CAD 2,874–14,369) to illustrate. Professional-association benchmarks sit at the top of that range or above it. Self-publishers almost always pay a flat fee in instalments. Under Canadian law, any transfer of rights must be in writing and signed, and moral rights can only be waived, never assigned.

### Cited Findings
**Price levels**
- Reedsy marketplace ("thousands of quotes"):
  - Single page: USD 60–200 (CAD 86–287).
  - Two-page spread: USD 100–400 (CAD 144–575).
  - Full picture book: USD 2,000–10,000.
  - Paid art test (character sheet or one scene): usually under USD 200.
  - Spot or half-page art lowers the cost.
  - [Reedsy, updated 2026-03-05](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/find-an-illustrator/)
- On Reedsy, the average illustrator charged USD 1,540–4,950 (CAD 2,213–7,113) for a **24-page** book over the past three years. That price included concepts, typography, interior design and cover — [Reedsy cost guide, 2025-10-14](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/cost-to-self-publish-childrens-books)
- Cover-only picture book projects on Reedsy have a median of USD 470 (CAD 675). Most picture book covers are made as part of the full-book contract — [Reedsy covers, undated ©2026](https://reedsy.com/resources/book-covers/childrens/)
- Australian Society of Authors rate card, FY2026/7 ("$", presumably AUD; reviewed Nov 2024, 3.3% increase July 2026):
  - Cover: $1,495.
  - Colour double-page spread: $1,035. Colour full page: $778. Colour half page: $547.
  - Colour quarter page or chapter head: $345 (black and white: $247).
  - 32-page picture book: **$6,409+**.
  - Add 20% if copyright is assigned. Add 20% for one-off flat-fee pieces. The illustrator keeps moral rights.
  - [ASA](https://www.asauthors.org.au/?p=4108)
- An agent at The Jacky Winter Group gives these self-published ranges: cover AUD 1,000–2,500, and a 24-page book "starting around 7000 (AUD)". Quotes far below industry rates are a warning sign — [Jacky Winter, 2025-08-22](https://www.jackywinter.com/dear-jacky/i-wrote-a-picture-book-time-to-find-an-illustrator)
- ALLi [PRE-2023]: "$2,000 and $10,000", "generally don't exceed $6,000" — [ALLi, 2021-11-08](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/successfully-self-publishing-childrens-books-alli-guide/)
- Unverified vendor guide: entry-level illustrators about £1,500–3,000, established ones £6,000–12,000 — [publishing.co.uk](https://www.publishing.co.uk/guides/childrens-book-illustrators/)

**Royalty vs flat fee**
- The ASA says the ideal is a commission plus royalties, but "self-publishing authors rarely" can pay royalties — [ASA](https://www.asauthors.org.au/?p=4108)
- Self-published projects "usually" pay a flat fee — [Jacky Winter](https://www.jackywinter.com/dear-jacky/i-wrote-a-picture-book-time-to-find-an-illustrator)
- Agent Susan Hawk says whether a flat fee is right "really depends on the particular situation". A written agreement is essential — [Jane Friedman, 2015, upd. 2025-02-18](https://janefriedman.com/self-publishing-childrens/)
- Unverified: forum illustrators suggest 5–8% (or 10%) of list price, and warn against a high royalty paired with a low advance — [SVS forum](https://forum.svslearn.com/post/121541)

**Contracts, deposits, kill fees, rights**
- The written agreement should set out — [Jacky Winter](https://www.jackywinter.com/dear-jacky/i-wrote-a-picture-book-time-to-find-an-illustrator):
  - A deposit, a milestone payment, and a final payment on delivery.
  - Deadlines for sketches, finals and revisions.
  - Which rights are licensed (the book only, or also merchandise, marketing and other formats). Broader use means a higher fee.
  - Typesetting and design are not the illustrator's job unless you agree and budget for them.
- Reedsy contracts cover dates, payments, disputes and IP. Two rights models are common — [Reedsy 2026-03-05](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/find-an-illustrator/):
  - Rights transfer on full payment, and the illustrator keeps portfolio use.
  - The illustrator keeps copyright in the art (not the characters) and grants indefinite use.
- Canadian Copyright Act (current to 2026-10-29) — [Copyright Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-42/page-3.html):
  - The author is the first owner (s.13(1)).
  - An employer is first owner only of works made in the course of employment (s.13(3)).
  - An assignment or licence is valid only "in writing signed by the owner of the right" (s.13(4)).
  - Moral rights "may not be assigned but may be waived" (s.14.1).
- Unverified templates:
  - 40–50% deposit.
  - Kill fee of 25% after sketches, 50% after colour roughs, 100% after finals.
  - Two sketch revision rounds and one minor round at finals.
  - Do not deliver finals before final payment.
  - [CUNY Pressbooks](https://pressbooks.cuny.edu/illustration/chapter/thinking-about-artist-contracts)
- Unverified: if the contract does not mention a kill fee, "there is no guarantee that one will be paid" — [GAG glossary](https://graphicartistsguild.org/contract-glossary)

**Where to find illustrators, and the risks**
- The CANSCAIP directory can be filtered by province and by craft (Illustrator, Editor, Translator and others). Profiles link to members' websites — [CANSCAIP](https://canscaip.org/Members)
- SCBWI runs an Illustrator Gallery. Members must remove GenAI content from the gallery and bookstore by Dec 31, 2026 — [SCBWI, rev. Sept 2026](https://www.scbwi.org/faq/global/scbwi-policies)
- Platform differences — [Reedsy 2026-03-05](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/find-an-illustrator/):
  - Reedsy is vetted (300+ illustrators), with default contracts and up to 5 quotes.
  - On Upwork and Fiverr, "none of the freelancers are vetted".
  - On Behance and Dribbble, you negotiate your own rights contract.
- Agents "often don't handle self-published projects". Look on social media, artists' websites, SCBWI and Illustrators for Hire — [Jacky Winter](https://www.jackywinter.com/dear-jacky/i-wrote-a-picture-book-time-to-find-an-illustrator)

**Process.** Stages: character design, storyboarding, page layout, illustration, colouring, file formatting, with "a few rounds" of storyboarding, designing and colouring — [Reedsy 2026-03-05](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/find-an-illustrator/). Timing is covered in §8.

**File specs**
- KDP — [KDP trim/bleed](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6):
  - Bleed is 0.125" (3.2 mm). Page size is trim + 0.125" in width and + 0.25" in height.
  - Outside margins must be at least 0.375" with bleed. If any page bleeds, the whole interior needs bleed.
  - **Hardcover in premium colour: 75–550 pages**. Premium colour paperback starts at 24 pages.
- KDP hardcover cover: art wraps 0.51" (15 mm). Keep text and images 0.635" (16 mm) from the edge. Images must be at least 300 DPI — [KDP cover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTKFJPNQCBTMRV6)
- Unverified (page returned 403): IngramSpark colour interiors need 300 ppi, CMYK total ink of 240% or less, and PDF/X-1a:2001. Bleed is 0.125" on the three trim edges only, never the binding side — [IngramSpark help](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/35306907207821-Interior-File-Creation)

**Canadian rate references**
- CARFAC-RAAV Minimum Recommended Fee Schedule — [CARFAC](https://www.carfac.ca/en/carfac-raav-minimum-recommended-fee-schedule):
  - Updated yearly and hosted on carfac-raav.ca.
  - Copyright fees and royalties are subject to GST.
  - The page I opened shows no book figures.
- Unverified (404): Illustration Québec publishes a rate grid and a model contract. Its code of ethics (art. 9) says tests requested before a commission must be paid — [IQ guide](https://illustrationquebec.com/media/news/posts/iq_fr_commenttravailleravecuneillustrateurtrice.pdf)

### Inferences
- A 32-page book is about 14 spreads plus a cover. Reedsy's per-spread range gives about USD 1,400–5,600 before the cover.
- ASA per-item rates add up to about AUD 16,000, against a whole-book floor of AUD 6,409+, so whole-book quotes are much cheaper than per-item pricing.
- Rough experience tiers, derived from the figures above:
  - Emerging or marketplace: USD 1,500–4,000.
  - Experienced kidlit: USD 4,000–8,000.
  - Trade or agency level: USD 8,000–15,000+.
- Offer royalties only if you can report sales transparently and the illustrator is effectively a co-creator. Otherwise, pay a flat fee, optionally plus a sales bonus.
- Canadian contract essentials:
  - A signed assignment or licence that names formats, territory and term, including the French edition, ebook, video read-alouds and merchandise.
  - A moral-rights waiver covering cropping and text placement.
  - Credit terms.
  - A warranty that no GenAI was used.
  - Delivery of text-free, layered masters.

### Gaps
- Illustration Québec's amounts, the CARFAC-RAAV book figures and the Graphic Artists Guild Handbook figures (paid) were not accessible. I could not confirm that "Illustrators Canada" exists, because the search budget ran out.

## 2. Editing

### Takeaway
Picture books are priced per project and minimum fees dominate: about USD 230–700 per pass. A 10,000-word chapter book runs about USD 660–880 (CAD 948–1,264) for a developmental edit, copyedit and proofread.

### Cited Findings
- EFA (survey Nov 2025–Jan 2026, 2025 rates; the EFA "neither sets rates nor advises") — [EFA, 2026-05-04](https://www.the-efa.org/rates/):

| Service | Picture book | Middle grade |
|---|---|---|
| Developmental | 3.6–4.5¢/word ($60–65/h) | 3.0–3.8¢/word |
| Line edit | 3.5–5.0¢/word | 2.8–4.0¢/word |
| Copyedit | 2.5–3.5¢/word ($50–60/h) | 2.3–3.0¢/word |
| Proofread | 2.3–4.3¢/word | 1.3–2.0¢/word |

  Manuscript assessment: 1.5–1.8¢/word ($40–54/h).
- Reedsy averages:
  - Picture book (~1,000 words): copyedit USD 231 (CAD 332), developmental USD 374 (CAD 537).
  - Middle grade (~30,000 words): copyedit USD 671, developmental USD 913.
  - [Reedsy 2025-10-14](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/cost-to-self-publish-childrens-books)
- Kidlit editors' published prices:
  - Mary Kole: picture book USD 499+ (text only), USD 699+ (with illustrations); page undated — [Kole](https://marykole.squarespace.com/editorial-service-rates)
  - Harold Underdown: developmental editing USD 150/h with a USD 500 minimum. No copyediting or proofreading — [Underdown, Mar 2026](https://underdown.org/rates.htm)
- Editors Canada 2022 survey [PRE-2023] (fielded Feb–Mar 2022; 220 respondents, 123 reporting hourly rates; presumably CAD):
  - Most respondents charged $40–59/h.
  - Proofreading: 31% under $40, 45% $40–59, 19% $60–79, 5% $80+.
  - Copy editing: 22% / 44% / 24% / 9%.
  - Developmental editing: 15% / 39% / 30% / 16%.
  - 74% charged no more than $60/h for book publishing work.
  - [Editors Canada PDF](https://editors.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Editors-Canada-2022-Membership-Survey-Report_final-edits-with-Alt-text_08OCT2023.pdf)
- Picture books run about 1,000 words or fewer. Self-published manuscripts are often too long and too didactic — [Jane Friedman](https://janefriedman.com/self-publishing-childrens/)

### Inferences
- Per-word maths misleads for picture books: 750 words × 3.6–4.5¢ is only USD 27–34, so minimums decide the price.
- A 10,000-word chapter book at EFA middle-grade rates:
  - Developmental: USD 300–380 (CAD 431–546).
  - Line edit: USD 280–400.
  - Copyedit: USD 230–300 (CAD 330–431).
  - Proofread: USD 130–200 (CAD 187–287).
- A Canadian copyeditor at CAD 40–59/h, working at the EFA pace of 8–11 pages/h (250 words per page), would take about 4–5 h, or roughly CAD 145–295.
- Picture book editing focuses on page-turn pacing, read-aloud meter and art notes. The proofread has to happen on laid-out pages with the art in place, which is why Kole prices "with illustrations" separately.

### Gaps
- I found no Editors Canada rate data from 2023–2026, and no Canadian per-project kidlit rates.

## 3. Design and Layout

### Takeaway
Picture book layout costs USD 250 to about 2,000. Affinity (now free) or InDesign handle spreads, bleed and CMYK. Atticus cannot do full-bleed spreads.

### Cited Findings
- A dedicated interior designer costs USD 250–750 (CAD 359–1,078). Text-based children's novels can be formatted free in Reedsy Studio — [Reedsy 2025-10-14](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/cost-to-self-publish-childrens-books)
- EFA 2025 — [EFA](https://www.the-efa.org/rates/):
  - Picture book formatting: $60–80/h, or $33.13–64.38 per page. That is USD 1,060–2,060 (CAD 1,523–2,960) for 32 pages.
  - Book layout: $50–55/h. Cover design: $50/h.
- Editors Canada 2022 [PRE-2023], desktop publishing and layout: 23% charged under $40/h, 36% $40–59/h, 41% $60–79/h — [Editors Canada](https://editors.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Editors-Canada-2022-Membership-Survey-Report_final-edits-with-Alt-text_08OCT2023.pdf)
- Affinity — [Lulu blog, early 2026](https://blog.lulu.com/affinity-by-canva/):
  - "Canva opted to make Affinity completely free." You only need a Canva account.
  - Lulu's IDML templates open in it. Master pages, spreads and CMYK export are supported.
  - Lulu requires single-page PDFs.
- Atticus says it is "not currently the perfect solution for picture books". It allows full bleed "but not currently as a spread", so you place two images side by side — [Atticus, 2026-03-09](https://intercom.help/atticus-5877e36564df/en/articles/12684319-does-atticus-support-children-s-books)
- Typesetting and design are not part of an illustrator's job unless you budget for them — [Jacky Winter](https://www.jackywinter.com/dear-jacky/i-wrote-a-picture-book-time-to-find-an-illustrator)
- Unverified:
  - Vellum has no fixed-layout EPUB and is built for reflowable novels — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12003)
  - Canva's CMYK export needs Pro, and raster images may not convert — [PrintNinja](https://printninja.com/setting-up-your-files-in-canva/)

### Inferences
- Use Affinity or InDesign for a picture book. Atticus or Vellum are fine for a chapter book or collection with spot art.
- Get several quotes covering the interior, the hardcover case wrap and a fixed-layout ebook. The two benchmarks above differ by more than 2×.

### Gaps
- I could not open Vellum's or Canva's official documentation.

## 4. Sensitivity / Authenticity Readers

### Takeaway
A reader is recommended when depicting identities or experiences outside your own. Per-word rates are about 1–1.8¢, but for a picture book the reader's minimum fee decides the price.

### Cited Findings
- EFA 2025: sensitivity reading 1.2–1.5¢/word ($50–60/h); authenticity 1.0–1.5¢; both combined 1.5–1.8¢ — [EFA](https://www.the-efa.org/rates/)
- Writer's Digest [PRE-2023]:
  - Salt & Sage starts at $90 per 10,000 words.
  - Writing Diversely starts at "$.0008 per word" (probably a typo) with a $75 minimum. "A penny a word is the norm."
  - Recommended when the writing is outside the writer's experience. Covers race, disability, mental health, immigration and more.
  - [WD 2022-06-29](https://writersdigest.com/write-better-fiction/who-are-sensitivity-editors-and-how-much-does-sensitivity-reading-pay)
- Unverified: $150 per picture book — [Meikle rate card](https://ashleighmeikle.com.au/editing-services/)

### Inferences
- A 10,000-word chapter book costs about USD 120–180 (CAD 172–259).
- For a picture book, expect a minimum of roughly USD 75–150 or more. Have the reader review the sketches (skin tones, hair, clothing, cultural objects) as well as the text, before finals.

### Gaps
- I found no Canadian survey of sensitivity reader fees.

## 5. French Translation (Canadian Market)

### Takeaway
The ATTLC recommends $0.25/word for prose and $0.35 for poetry (presumably CAD; the page shows "$" only). Minimum fees and the work of re-creating rhyme will dominate the cost of a picture book. Federal translation grants appear to be open only to established publishers.

### Cited Findings
- ATTLC/LTAC recommends $0.25/word for "other genres", $0.28 for drama and $0.35 for poetry, as negotiable base rates. There is no children's book rate. It refers to the UNEQ grid (2020–2021). The page's 2026–27 validity dates apply to event rates, not translation — [ATTLC](https://www.attlc-ltac.org/en/rates/)
- EFA 2025: translation from English 12–19¢/word (USD) — [EFA](https://www.the-efa.org/rates/)
- The Canada Council's Translation component funds Canadian "literary works or dramatic works" into French, English, Indigenous languages and ASL/LSQ. Deadlines are Jan 13, Jun 16 and Oct 13, 2027. International Translation is for organizations and international publishers — [Canada Council](https://canadacouncil.ca/funding/grants/arts-across-canada/translation)
- Unverified: applicants must be literary publishers that already receive core funding, and self-published works are excluded — [third-party listing](https://fundsforindividuals.fundsforngos.org/grants/funding-for-translation-of-canadian-literary-works-or-dramatic-works/)
- Unverified [PRE-2023]: OTTIAQ surveys put the average general translation rate at about $0.22/word in 2008–2012 — [OTTIAQ 2018](https://sondage.ottiaq.org/app/uploads/2019/07/resultats_sondage2018_fr.pdf)
- KDP requires translations to credit the translator and the author, and AI-generated translations must be disclosed — [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390)
- The CANSCAIP directory can be filtered for translators — [CANSCAIP](https://canscaip.org/Members)
- Unverified: the French *Gruffalo* lost its rhyme because the art constrained the wording — [Library Mice](https://librarymice.com/my-pictures-after-the-storm-and-the-art-of-translation/)

### Inferences
- At ATTLC rates:
  - 700-word prose picture book: CAD 175.
  - Rhyming text at the poetry rate: CAD 245.
  - 10,000-word chapter book: CAD 2,500 (EFA gives USD 1,200–1,900).
- Rhyme needs adaptation rather than literal translation. Pay a flat fee or the poetry rate, let the translator rewrite, credit them on the cover, and budget for relaying the text.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm current Canada Council eligibility rules, a post-2018 OTTIAQ survey, the UNEQ amounts, or a practitioner guide to adapting rhyme.

## 6. AI-Generated Illustration (KDP rules, copyright, reception, disclosure)

### Takeaway
KDP requires you to disclose AI-generated images but hides that disclosure from readers. Prompt-only art is not copyrightable in the US, and Canadian law is unsettled. SCBWI and the CCBC exclude AI illustration, and booksellers and librarians screen for it.

### Cited Findings
**KDP**
- Disclose AI-generated text, images (cover and interior) and translations when you publish or republish. "AI-generated" applies even after substantial edits.
- AI-assisted content (your own work that AI edited or refined, or ideas you brainstormed with AI) does not need disclosure. You remain responsible for IP compliance. The page shows no date and no title limit.
- [KDP Content Guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390)
- "Amazon keeps that disclosure to itself" — [Valley News 2026-10-02](https://vnews.com/2026/10/02/ai-book-controversy-claremont/)

**Copyright**
- US Copyright Office, Part 2 (released 2025-01-29, [USCO AI page](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/)) — [USCO Part 2](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf):
  - No copyright in "purely AI-generated material, or material where there is insufficient human control".
  - "prompts do not alone provide sufficient control."
  - Perceptible human expression, creative selection and arrangement, and creative modifications are protected.
  - Assistive use of AI does not affect protection.
  - The 2023 guidance asks applicants to disclose more than de minimis AI material.
- *Zarya of the Dawn* (2023-02-21): the registration covered the human text and the "selection, coordination, and arrangement", but not the Midjourney images — [USCO Part 2, fn. 11](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf)
- *Thaler v. Perlmutter*: the D.C. Circuit ruled on 2025-03-18, and certiorari was denied on 2026-03-02 — [SCOTUS docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/25-449.html)
- Canada: CIPO registration No. 1188619 lists a human and RAGHAV AI as co-authors. *CIPPIC v. Sahni* (T-1717-24, filed 2024-07-08) seeks expungement. Memoranda were filed in mid-2025, a hearing date is pending, and there is no decision — [CIPPIC](https://www.cippic.ca/our-work/cippic-v-sahni)
- Unverified: ISED's "What We Heard" report on copyright and GenAI came out 2025-02-11, with no policy decided — [ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/strategic-policy-sector/en/node/455)

**Reception**
- SCBWI (policy from 2023, revised Sept 2026) is "opposed to any use of text-prompted generative AI in writing, illustrating, translating". Awards and grants require a no-GenAI certification, and entries can be disqualified — [SCBWI](https://www.scbwi.org/faq/global/scbwi-policies)
- CCBC 2026 awards: "Works containing AI-authored content, in part or in whole, or AI-generated illustrations, are not eligible." Minor research or formatting use "may be permitted". The fee is $40 per title per award, and creators must be citizens or permanent residents of at least 2 years — [CCBC 2026-02-06](https://bookcentre.ca/blogs/industry-news/call-for-submissions-2026-ccbc-book-awards)
- New Zealand: the Ockham awards removed two books over AI covers ("judged as a whole — including jacket design") — [RNZ 2025-11-18](https://www.rnz.co.nz/life/books/top-writers-ruled-out-of-nz-book-awards-due-to-ai-covers). The children's awards bar AI text in 2026 and will rule on illustrations for 2027 — [NZSA 2025-11-27](https://authors.org.nz/entries-open-for-2026-nzcya-awards/)
- Canadian Press headline: "Canada's literary awards say publishers are in best position to sniff out AI" (body not retrievable) — [CTV 2026-09-25](https://www.ctvnews.ca/entertainment/article/canadas-literary-awards-say-publishers-are-in-best-position-to-sniff-out-ai/). Unverified: Hackmatack excludes AI text and illustrations, including covers — [Hackmatack](https://hackmatack.ca/downloads/nominating/2026-27EnglishCallForSubmissions.pdf)
- Booksellers: Great Lakes Independent Booksellers Association members refuse to stock suspected AI books. Prologue Bookshop has an anti-AI policy. Wild Rumpus declines self-published pitches that use AI — [Mother Jones 2025-11-26](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2025/11/ai-childrens-books/)
- Libraries — [American Libraries 2025-09-02](https://americanlibrariesmagazine.org/2025/09/02/books-by-bots/):
  - Librarians vet more after bad AI children's titles. They check author footprints and Copyright Office records.
  - Hoopla relies on vendor metadata. Collection-development AI policies are rare.
- Parents — NC State/McMaster study (13 parent–child groups) — [EurekAlert 2025-11-13](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1105923):
  - Most parents accept AI images if a human wrote the text and experts reviewed the images.
  - Children notice emotional mismatches.
  - Parents want "a simple cover label, not page-level flags".
- Backlash:
  - Claremont, NH: errors in a self-published book's AI art. A local author called the school's purchase "a slap in the face" and urged disclosure on the copyright page — [Valley News](https://vnews.com/2026/10/02/ai-book-controversy-claremont/)
  - *Alice and Sparkle* [PRE-2023] — [Kirkus 2023-04-27](https://kirkusreviews.com/writers-center/publishing/debate-over-using-ai-generated-art-books)
- Unverified: the Authors Guild's "Human Authored" mark (text only) can go on the copyright page — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/human-authored/usage-guidelines/)

### Inferences
- If you use AI: disclose on KDP, state it on the copyright page and with a cover label, and register only the human text and arrangement.
- To protect award eligibility, SCBWI standing, bookstore placement and library goodwill, avoid GenAI art. Require illustrators to warrant in writing that no GenAI was used.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether KDP's former title-per-day cap still applies, the AI policies of Canadian libraries and chains, or the Governor General's Awards' policy.

## 7. Typical Total Budgets (32-page picture book)

### Takeaway
Sourced aggregates run from about USD 2,400 (Reedsy averages) to over AUD 6,400 for illustration alone at professional rates. I did not find or open any published first-person author budget.

### Cited Findings
- Editing, illustration, cover and formatting combined: USD 2,387–4,400 (CAD 3,430–6,322) — [Reedsy 2025-10-14](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/cost-to-self-publish-childrens-books)
- "At least $5,000" [PRE-2023] — [ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/successfully-self-publishing-childrens-books-alli-guide/)

## 8. Timeline Norms

### Takeaway
Sources point to 6–12+ months of lead time. About 9–15 months from final manuscript to launch is realistic for an indie picture book; that range is my inference.

### Cited Findings
- About a year from concept to completion; good illustrators may have waits of weeks or months [PRE-2023] — [ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/successfully-self-publishing-childrens-books-alli-guide/)
- Illustration takes "several months" before design and production. With a fixed date, plan 6–12 months ahead — [Jacky Winter 2025](https://www.jackywinter.com/dear-jacky/i-wrote-a-picture-book-time-to-find-an-illustrator)
- Book professionals 4+ months ahead — [Reedsy 2025](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/cost-to-self-publish-childrens-books)
- Unverified: illustration takes 4–6 months; a traditional picture book takes 18–24 months — [Penguin UK](https://www.penguin.co.uk/articles/company/getting-published/Illustrating-a-childrens-book.html)

### Inferences
- Sequence: editing (1–2 months), the illustrator's waitlist and contract, character design and storyboard, sketches, finals (the bulk), then layout and proofs.
- A chapter book with spot art is faster.

## Gotchas
1. **A KDP premium colour hardcover needs at least 75 pages**, so a 32-page hardcover is impossible on KDP. You would need IngramSpark or an offset printer, and the printing researcher should confirm this — [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6)
2. In Canada, a copyright assignment must be written and signed, and moral rights can only be waived. A "work for hire" email is not enough — [Copyright Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-42/page-3.html)
3. Machine translation (for example AI French) counts as "AI-generated" on KDP and must be disclosed — [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390)
4. Readers never see KDP's AI disclosure — [Valley News](https://vnews.com/2026/10/02/ai-book-controversy-claremont/). Librarians check Copyright Office records instead — [American Libraries](https://americanlibrariesmagazine.org/2025/09/02/books-by-bots/)
5. AI illustrations make a book ineligible for CCBC awards and for SCBWI awards and grants — [CCBC](https://bookcentre.ca/blogs/industry-news/call-for-submissions-2026-ccbc-book-awards); [SCBWI](https://www.scbwi.org/faq/global/scbwi-policies)
6. Per-word editing rates are meaningless for picture books; minimums of USD 500 and up are common — [Underdown](https://underdown.org/rates.htm)
7. Atticus cannot do full-bleed spreads — [Atticus](https://intercom.help/atticus-5877e36564df/en/articles/12684319-does-atticus-support-children-s-books). Canva's CMYK and Vellum's fixed-layout limits are unverified.
8. USD quotes cost about 1.44× in CAD — [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/). Add GST/HST on Canadian quotes.
9. Agents rarely take self-published commissions, and very low quotes are a red flag — [Jacky Winter](https://www.jackywinter.com/dear-jacky/i-wrote-a-picture-book-time-to-find-an-illustrator). Marketplace freelancers are unvetted — [Reedsy](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/how-to-self-publish-a-childrens-book/find-an-illustrator/)
10. Federal translation grants appear closed to self-publishers (unverified), so budget French yourself.
11. Picture book layout quotes vary more than 2×: Reedsy USD 250–750 vs EFA about USD 1,060–2,060 — [EFA](https://www.the-efa.org/rates/)
