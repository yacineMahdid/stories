# Marketing a self-published children's book on Amazon, and Canadian support (as of 2026-11-24)

Method: I opened every cited page on 2026-11-24 unless it is flagged. "Unverified" means I saw it only in a search snippet, or the page blocked access (403/503). Canadian programs are in CAD and US services in USD. The shared web-search budget ran out before every item was checked (see the final list).

## 1. Pre-launch and reviews (ARCs, paid reviews, Canadian venues, Amazon rules)

### Takeaway
Paid US reviews cost USD 59–750. The CCBC's *Book News* and *Best Books* accept self-published titles; Quill & Quire reportedly doesn't (unverified). Amazon allows free ARCs, but you can't require a review, steer it, or add incentives.

### Cited Findings
- **Kirkus Indie (USD):**
  - Picture Book review: $399, ~200 words ($450 expedited).
  - Traditional review: $450 ($599 expedited). Expanded review: $599 ($750 expedited).
  - Turnaround: 7–9 weeks standard, 4–6 weeks express.
  - You can keep the review private. Self-published, unpublished and already-published books are accepted.
  - A Kirkus Star makes the book eligible for the Kirkus Prize.
  - Source: [Kirkus Indie](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/indie-reviews/purchase/). The page shows 2026 content but no price date.
- **BookLife/PW (USD; guidelines dated 2025-05-21):**
  - PW review consideration: $25, not guaranteed, takes 12+ weeks, digital copies only, book must be sold in the US.
  - BookLife Reviews: $399, guaranteed within 6 weeks (4 weeks expedited), private unless you publish it.
  - Source: [BookLife](https://booklife.com/about-us/review-submission-guidelines.html)
- **Foreword Clarion:** The page returned 403. Snippets give $579 USD for 400 words in 4–6 weeks, non-cancellable; third-party guides cite $499. Unverified. Sources: [Foreword](https://publishers.forewordreviews.com/reviews); [Clarion terms](https://publishers.forewordreviews.com/reviews/clarion/terms)
- **Readers' Favorite (USD):** A free review takes ~10–12 weeks and isn't guaranteed. Express costs $59 (2 weeks), $129 for 3 or $199 for 5. Reviews under 4 stars aren't posted. Source: [Readers' Favorite](https://readersfavorite.com/book-reviews.htm)
- **Midwest Book Review:** Print copies are considered free (2 copies plus cover letter and press release; ~12–14 weeks), with "priority consideration to... self-published authors". A $50 USD Reader Fee, paid to the reviewer, guarantees review of ebooks and ARCs. The policy is dated 2014. Source: [MBR](https://midwestbookreview.com/get_rev.htm)
- **IndieReader (USD):** Standard review $299 (6–8 weeks); rush $399 (3–5 weeks). A 4–5 star rating earns "IR Approved". Source: [IndieReader](https://indiereader.com/?p=64570)
- **Canadian Children's Book News (CCBC):**
  - Eligibility is based on the creator: the book must be "written OR illustrated by a Canadian". No publisher-type rule is stated.
  - AI-generated content, including covers, is ineligible.
  - Submit a PDF or EPUB to shannon@bookcentre.ca. It receives 700+ submissions a year and covers 120–150. No fee is stated.
  - Source: [CCBN](https://bookcentre.ca/pages/canadian-childrens-book-news)
- **CCBC Best Books for Kids & Teens (Fall 2025 call; no later call seen):**
  - Self-published imprints are eligible with a valid ISBN. Physical books only.
  - Send 8 copies plus a PDF. The deadline was 2025-06-13, and no fee is stated.
  - Senders using US printers pay all shipping and duty.
  - Source: [BBKT](https://bookcentre.ca/pages/bbkt-call-for-submissions)
- **Quill & Quire:** "usually does not review professional, educational, or self-published titles". Unverified (403). Source: [Q&Q FAQ](https://quillandquire.com/?p=45040)
- **CM: Canadian Review of Materials:** Reviews Canadian-connected children's and YA titles; volunteer-run; weekly from September to June. I found no policy on self-published books. Unverified (403). Source: [CM](https://www.cmreviews.ca/node/10)
- **Amazon review rules:**
  - "You may provide free or discounted copies... as long as you do not require a review in exchange" or try to influence it.
  - "Offering anything other than a free or discounted copy of the book, including gift cards, will invalidate a review." Paid ("compensation") reviews are removed.
  - Source: [KDP Customer Reviews](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202101910) (undated). The Community Guidelines page returned 503 on both .com and .ca.
  - ARC senders must follow the "Customer Review Creation Guidelines for Paid Reviews". Unverified. Source: [KDP Merchandising](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200673650)

### Inferences
- Order reviews 3–4 months before launch, since PW takes 12+ weeks and Kirkus standard takes 7–9.
- A free CCBN review or BBKT listing probably counts for more with Canadian librarians than a paid US review. Libraries discount paid reviews (§3).
- ARCs: give copies with no conditions attached and no gifts. I could not confirm Amazon's exact disclosure wording.

### Gaps
- Amazon Community Guidelines text on ARC disclosure.
- Current Q&Q and CM policies.
- Whether Foreword's free review program accepts self-published books.

## 2. Amazon marketing (Ads, A+, Author Central, promotions, categories/keywords, launch velocity)

### Takeaway
Amazon.ca supports KDP ads and A+ Content (English and French). You get 3 categories and 7 keywords. There's no children's-specific benchmark: Books average about USD 0.38 CPC and 19% ACOS (vendor data). New books get a temporary boost, and sustained sales beat spikes.

### Cited Findings
- **KDP ads:**
  - Available in the "US, Canada, Mexico, UK, Germany, Italy, Spain, France, Netherlands, India, Australia, and Japan".
  - Sponsored Products are pay-per-click. Sponsored Brands need a pen name with 3 titles.
  - Adding a payment method places a $50 pre-authorization hold. Each marketplace bills separately.
  - Source: [KDP ads help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201499010)
- **Amazon's author guide:** Start with automatic targeting, then add manual and negative targets. Review every ~2 weeks. Budgets are averaged monthly (example: $10/day), and dynamic bids can rise up to +100% for top-of-search. Source: [Amazon Ads guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/authors-guide-to-sponsored-products)
- **Benchmarks (USD; Ad Badger app data, Jan–Aug 2026; page updated 2026-09-10):**
  - Books: CPC $0.38, CVR 18%, ACOS 19%, ~$2.11 ad cost per sale.
  - Toys & Games: CPC $0.78, ACOS 28%, seasonal October–December.
  - All categories: CPC $1.22, ACOS 29.6%.
  - Not children's-specific and not Amazon.ca.
  - Source: [Ad Badger](https://www.adbadger.com/blog/amazon-advertising-stats/)
- **Gaughran/Inglis (2022):** Advertise only after you have good reviews, start with low bids, and advertise both ebook and print. Source: [Gaughran](https://davidgaughran.com/marketing-kids-books-children-author-guide/)
- **A+ Content:**
  - Amazon.ca supports English and French. You must publish separately in each marketplace, though drafts can be copied.
  - Content goes live about 8 business days after approval. Images must be at least 300 DPI. Cost is not stated.
  - Source: [KDP A+](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G8EP5W6H9CY7T8GS)
- **Categories:** "can select 3 categories". Children's categories need a reading age of 0–2, 3–5, 6–8 or 9–12. Categories differ by marketplace and format, and changes take up to 72 hours. Source: [KDP Categories](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652170)
- **Keywords:** Up to 7, ideally 2–3-word phrases; avoid subjective or time-sensitive terms. Unverified. Source: [KDP Keywords](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201298500)
- **Kindle Countdown Deals:**
  - Available only on Amazon.com and Amazon.co.uk, so not on Amazon.ca.
  - Ebooks only, in KDP Select for 30+ days with Amazon exclusivity. List price must be $2.99–$24.99.
  - A deal runs 1 hour to 7 days. Each 90-day term allows either one Countdown Deal or Free Book Promotion days, not both.
  - Source: [KDP KCD](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201293780)
- **Author Central:** I found no evidence it has merged into KDP. Bio links were reportedly removed around March 2025. Unverified. Source: [A Marketing Expert](https://amarketingexpert.com/2025/03/27/amazon-news-author-central-changes-and-search-result-trends/)
- **Launch velocity (Kindlepreneur, 2025-11-08):** Across 74 new books, there was a "honeymoon" boost: more keyword rankings than older books with similar sales. Strong, consistent launch sales lengthened it, and visibility fell afterward. The author advises a ~2-week multi-tactic launch, then ongoing ads and email. The sample is small. Source: [Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com/does-amazon-give-preferential-treatment-to-new-books/)
- **ALLi:** Sales rank weighs recency: 5 sales a day for 2 weeks outranks 3,000 on launch day. Unverified. Source: [ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/amazon-sales-rank-taming-the-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- Break-even ACOS = royalty ÷ list price. Thin picture-book royalties put break-even below the 19% Books average, so treat early ads as discovery spend.
- Amazon.ca campaigns are separate and billed separately, and Countdown Deals are unavailable there.
- **Myth vs reality:** The myth is that a launch spike locks in the algorithm. In reality the boost is temporary and rewards sustained sales, so spread launch tactics over 2–4 weeks.

### Gaps
- Children's-specific and Amazon.ca CPC/ACOS figures, and typical budgets.
- The Free Book Promotion day limit (not stated on the page).
- Price benchmarks for children's books.

## 3. Off-Amazon marketing (social, influencers, TpT, local media, libraries, bookstores, email/CASL)

### Takeaway
Children's books sell mostly offline: through schools, libraries, parents and word of mouth. Online marketing has to target adults. A Canadian email list must follow CASL.

### Cited Findings
- **Gaughran/Inglis (2022):** Early sales come offline (libraries, bookshops, schools, playgroups). Send local press releases with a local angle. Children under 13 can't be reached online, and parents buy on recommendations. Run a parent email list with a freebie, sent monthly. Source: [Gaughran](https://davidgaughran.com/marketing-kids-books-children-author-guide/)
- **Inglis (ALLi, 2016):** School events are the "top route" to readers. YouTube readings (tagged so they appear in YouTube Kids) and giveaways for reviews also help; Toppsta (UK) charges £100 + VAT for 12 books. Source: [ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/how-to-market-childrens-books/)
- **Bookstagram:** Use #bookstagram, #childrensbooks and #picturebooks, and offer influencers free copies. Unverified. Source: [Write Practice](https://thewritepractice.com/market-your-childrens-book/)
- **Teachers Pay Teachers (USD presumed):**
  - Basic: $29 once, 55% payout, $0.30 per resource sold.
  - Exclusive: $59.95/yr, 77.5% payout (80% introductory "until January 1"), and digital products must be sold only on TpT.
  - The page doesn't say whether non-US sellers can join.
  - Source: [TpT](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Become-A-Seller)
- **Toronto Public Library (updated 2026-11-19):**
  - It buys self-published books "very selectively", favouring those "of significant local interest or which have received media attention".
  - Send title, ISBN, price, binding, audience, illustration details and objective reviews (paid or customer reviews don't count). Don't send a copy.
  - TPL won't prepay or buy from your website, so a wholesaler may be needed.
  - Ebooks go through the Indie Author Project.
  - Source: [TPL](https://tpl.ca/about-the-library/business-with-the-library/self-published-authors/)
- **CASL (ISED page modified 2019-04-01):**
  - Express consent does not expire. Implied consent lasts up to 2 years after a purchase or 6 months after an inquiry.
  - Every message needs the sender's name, a mailing address and other contact details valid for 60 days, plus a free unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days.
  - Keep records of consent.
  - Source: [ISED CASL](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/node/7)
  - Pre-checked boxes don't count as consent. Unverified. Source: [fightspam](https://www.fightspam.gc.ca/eic/site/030.nsf/eng/00008.html)

### Inferences
- PLR pays only when a title is found in library catalogues (§5), so library acquisition pays twice.
- Use an unticked opt-in box at storytimes and visits, and keep a record of each consent.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced data on TikTok or Pinterest, influencer rates, Canadian local media, bookstore consignment (Indigo or independents), or storytime booking. The search budget ran out.

## 4. School visits

### Takeaway
Fees I saw run about CAD 300–400 + HST per in-person presentation, but these come from individual author listings (unverified). TWUC's subsidized readings pay CAD 300, are open only to members, and exclude school visits. Self-published authors can qualify as members.

### Cited Findings
- **TWUC National Public Readings:**
  - Members only. Pays $300 for a full reading and $150 for a partial one.
  - Up to 2 full readings per year (April–March), with travel up to $100 beyond 100 km.
  - "School or classroom visits" are ineligible. Applications are closed, and 2026 funding is "not confirmed".
  - Source: [TWUC NPR](https://writersunion.ca/node/17)
- **TWUC benefits** include "paid public readings and school presentations". Source: [TWUC](https://writersunion.ca/become-a-member)
- **TWUC Ontario Writers-in-the-Schools:** $300 per presentation, up to 3 per school per year. An older page said the program was unfunded. Unverified. Source: [TWUC WITS](https://writersunion.ca/node/545)
- **Author listings:** $350 + HST per presentation; $400 + HST for one session or $800 for two; virtual visits $300 + HST. Unverified. Source: [AuthorsBooking](https://authorsbooking.com/tag/ontario-based/)
- **CANSCAIP:** I found no fee guideline. Friends, the tier self-published authors join, are not listed in the directory. Source: [CANSCAIP](https://canscaip.org/JoinCANSCAIP)
- **Booking:** Contact the school's literacy coordinator or librarian; wholesaler distribution helps. One author's school visits sold ~4,000 print copies of a single title. Unverified. Source: [Creative Penn](https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2023/08/14/publishing-books-for-children-and-profitable-school-visits-with-tonya-ellis/)

### Inferences
- TWUC membership is the main route to subsidized readings; schools pay for the visits themselves. Quote separate in-person and virtual rates.

### Gaps
- Official CANSCAIP and TWUC fee schedules.
- Whether self-published authors can tour in TD Canadian Children's Book Week.
- Provincial artists-in-schools programs.

## 5. Canadian income programs (PLR, Access Copyright, Copibec, Canadian content)

### Takeaway
PLR does not exclude self-published books. An eligible picture book (ISBN, 24+ pages, published in the last 5 years) earned CAD 60.14 per library hit in 2025–26, with a $50 minimum and $4,500 maximum. Access Copyright is free to join with a commercially available print or digital work, but whether Payback covers self-published or digital-only works is unclear.

### Cited Findings
- **PLR eligibility:**
  - Creator must be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident.
  - The book needs an ISBN, at least 48 pages (24 for children's books), and publication within the last 5 years.
  - Up to 6 contributors, each contributing at least 10%.
  - Ineligible: activity books, calendars, colouring books, and educational-market books.
  - Self-published books are not excluded.
  - Sources: [PLR eligibility](https://publiclendingright.ca/eligibility) (refers to the 2026 PDF); [PLR 2025 PDF](https://publiclendingright.ca/-/media/Files/Plr/Eligibility/2025_PLR_Eligibility_Criteria.pdf)
- **PLR 2025 PDF:** Registration runs "15 February to 1 May" by mail (postmark counts). Up to 20 new titles a year; a picture-book illustrator may claim 50%. "$14.8 million will be distributed to over 18,000 registered individuals." Payment tapers 100/80/70/50% with time registered.
- **PLR payments (2025–26):** $60.14 per hit; maximum $4,500; minimum $50; paid mid-February; claim within 6 months; no payments after 25 years. Source: [PLR payments](https://publiclendingright.ca/payments)
- **How PLR is triggered:** The title must appear in sampled library catalogues; loans don't count. Source: [PLR overview](https://publiclendingright.ca/about/overview)
- **Access Copyright, joining:**
  - You must "own reproduction rights to at least one commercially available published work in print or digital format".
  - You must be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident living outside Quebec, aged 18+, and not affiliated with Copibec.
  - Joining is free. Payback begins the year after you join.
  - Source: [AC](https://accesscopyright.ca/creators/affiliating-with-access-copyright/)
- **Payback:**
  - Sign up by December 31 to be paid the next year.
  - 40% of the pool is a base payment shared equally. The other 60% goes only to creators who file a claim between April 1 and May 31.
  - Only works from the last 20 years count. Payment usually arrives in November, and is held until it reaches $25.
  - Source: [AC FAQ](https://accesscopyright.ca/creators/faqs-for-creator-affiliates/)
- **Conflicting Access Copyright snippets (unverified):**
  - Payback "only applies to works published in print format". Source: [2025 Payback FAQ](https://www.accesscopyright.ca/media/1346/2025_payback_faqs.pdf)
  - Publisher affiliation is unavailable to publishers of "only... self-published materials". Source: [AC publishers](https://www.accesscopyright.ca/publishers/)
- **Copibec:** Pays usage royalties split 50/50 between author and publisher by default, and keeps 15%. Lump-sum payments come every two years, plus royalties from 36 foreign agencies. Payment is made once the balance exceeds $100. Self-publishing is not addressed. Source: [Copibec](https://www.copibec.ca/en/royalties)

### Inferences
- A sole author-illustrator can claim 100% of PLR, and one hit ($60.14) already clears the $50 minimum.
- **"Canadian content":** None of these programs pays for Canadian content as such. Benefits depend on the creator's citizenship or residency (PLR, Access Copyright, the CCBC, Forest of Reading). Hackmatack is the exception: it uses Atlantic content as a tie-breaker (§7).

### Gaps
- PLR 2026 registration method and the 2026–27 rate.
- How Access Copyright and Copibec treat self-published titles (Access Copyright contact: affiliates@accesscopyright.ca).

## 6. Grants

### Takeaway
BCAC (CAD 7,500) and OAC (CAD 12,000, but no picture books) accept self-published authors through sales or TWUC membership. AFA excludes self-published projects. The Canada Council's rule is unconfirmed, and the Canada Book Fund is for publishers only.

### Cited Findings
- **Canada Council, Explore and Create:** The Artistic Creation stream (which replaced Research and Creation) is open to "Canadian artists, groups and arts organizations". You can apply any time before the project starts. No grant maximums are stated. Source: [CCA](https://canadacouncil.ca/funding/grants/explore-and-create)
- **Canada Council ineligible activities:** The list does not mention self-publishing. Source: [CCA ineligible](https://canadacouncil.ca/funding/grants/guide/apply-to-programs/general-list-of-ineligible-activities). A snippet claimed self-published publishing is ineligible; that is unverified and conflicts with the page. An archived 2007 rule required a professional publisher. Source: [archive](https://bac-lac.wayback.archive-it.org/web/20071115002216/http://www.canadacouncil.ca/grants/writing/ri127227329682968750.htm)
- **OAC Literary Creation Projects:**
  - $12,000 for Ontario residents.
  - Self-published route: sales of more than 400 copies of a self-published prose work within the 24 months before your first application, or full TWUC or League of Canadian Poets membership.
  - Children's picture books are NOT eligible.
  - The deadline (2026-09-10) has passed and no 2027 date is posted.
  - Source: [OAC](https://www.arts.on.ca/grants/literary-creation-projects-works-for-publication)
- **BCAC Creative Writers 2026/27:**
  - Level One up to $7,500; Level Two $15,000. Closed 2026-10-08; next intake August 2027.
  - Self-published route: "Sales of more than 400 copies of at least two self-published and eligible works of prose, children's picture books, or graphic novels", with the latest published within 24 months. Alternatively, voting membership in TWUC, the CAA (professional) or the LCP (full).
  - "Self-published" includes hybrid publishers and self-publishing service providers.
  - Source: [BCAC guidelines](https://www.bcartscouncil.ca/app/uploads/sites/508/2026/09/FY2026-27-IAG-Creative-Writers-Program-Guidelines.pdf)
- **AFA Literary Individual Project:**
  - Up to $18,000.
  - Ineligible: "privately-printed, self-published, and vanity press publications", print-on-demand, and publication costs.
  - Requires 1 year of Alberta residency. Deadlines: 2027-03-01 and 2027-09-01.
  - Source: [AFA](https://affta.ab.ca/node/101)
- **CALQ (Quebec):** An archived program guide from about 2010 (search snippet, BAnQ collections) did not recognize self-publishing. Unverified and outdated. UNEQ publishes a grants guide for "auteur autonome" (self-published) authors, which I did not open. Source: [UNEQ guide](https://www.uneq.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/AUTEUR_AUTONOME_Guide_bourses-subventions.pdf)
- **Canada Book Fund (page modified 2026-02-18):**
  - "Only book publishing firms are eligible": 12+ months of operation, 15 trade titles, and at least $50,000 in net sales.
  - Self-published titles may not exceed 50% of new titles, and author-subsidized titles are ineligible.
  - Source: [CBF](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/services/funding/book-fund/publishing-support/application-guidelines.html)

### Inferences
- TWUC membership is the shortcut to grant eligibility in BC and Ontario. Even so, Ontario picture-book authors can't use OAC Literary Creation Projects. A one-book debut rarely meets BC's two-works sales test.

### Gaps
- The Canada Council's current applicant-profile rule for self-published writers.
- Current CALQ rules.
- The other provinces and municipal councils. (A snippet says the Toronto Arts Council excludes self-publishing; unverified.)

## 7. Awards

The TD Award ended in 2024. CCBC awards accept self-published books with an ISBN and Canadian distribution, and Hackmatack welcomes them. Forest of Reading bars them, and the GG Awards likely do too (unverified). Indie awards accept them for a fee.

### Cited Findings
- **TD Award:** 2024 was its "final year". Source: [CCBC 2025 call](https://bookcentre.ca/news/call-for-submissions-2025-ccbc-book-awards) (2024-12-06)
- **CCBC 2026 awards (call dated 2026-02-06):**
  - Marilyn Baillie (picture book), Geoffrey Bilson, David Booth, Richard Allen Chase, Sharon Fitzhenry (Canadian publishers only), and Amy Mathers.
  - Jean Little is now biennial; Arlene Barlin is retired.
  - Fee: $40 per title per award. Forms were due 2026-02-28, with 5 copies by 2026-03-27, for 2025 books. The 2027 call has not been seen.
  - Source: [CCBC 2026](https://bookcentre.ca/blogs/industry-news/call-for-submissions-2026-ccbc-book-awards)
- **Marilyn Baillie Award (ages 3–8; page updated Nov 2024):** Self-published books are eligible with a valid ISBN, and must be distributed in Canada. Creators must be Canadian citizens or permanent residents (2+ years). Board books are excluded. Source: [Baillie](https://bookcentre.ca/pages/marilyn-baillie-picture-book-award-eligibility-guidelines-fees)
- **GG Literary Awards 2027:**
  - Books published 2026-05-01 to 2027-04-30; deadline 2027-05-05.
  - Winners get $25,000, publishers $3,000, finalists $1,000. Categories include Young People's Literature Text and Illustrated Books.
  - The page doesn't mention self-publishing. Source: [GG](https://canadacouncil.ca/funding/prizes/governor-generals-literary-awards/guidelines-governor-generals-literary-awards)

Blue Spruce's 2027 cut-off is 2027-04-01. Source: [Forest](https://forestofreading.com/submission-procedure-selection-criteria/)

  - For BC-resident creators of children's fiction published within the last 2 years. Books must be "available in retail outlets and bookstores across BC".
  - Authors may submit their own books: $30 and 6 copies, by 2027-01-31.
  - Source: [Chocolate Lily](https://chocolatelilyawards.com/for-publishers)
- **Hackmatack (ages 8–11; call dated 2026-08-20):**
  - "from a Canadian publisher, or self-published by a Canadian author". Printed books published 2025–2026.

  - 6 copies; a $25 fee applies only if shortlisted. The deadline was 2026-10-15.
  - Source: [Hackmatack](https://hackmatack.ca/downloads/nominating/2027-28EnglishCallForSubmissions.pdf)
- **Red Cedar (grades 4–7):** Nominees are chosen from books "submitted by publishers"; I found no rule on self-published books. Unverified. Source: [BCTLA](https://bctla.ca/professional-development/red-cedar-awards/)
- **IPPY 2027 (dated 2026-07-27):**
  - Open to "self-publishers and independent authors throughout North America"; copyright years 2025–2027.
  - Fees (USD): $79 by 2026-10-14, $89 by 2026-12-16, $99 by 2027-03-04.
  - Picture-book categories: 7 & Under, and All Ages.
  - Source: [IPPY](https://ippyawards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IPPY-Guidelines-2027.pdf)
- **NGIBA 2027:** USD $80 for the first category and $65 for each additional; deadline 2027-02-12; open to self-published books. Unverified (403). Source: [NGIBA](https://indiebookawards.com/faq.php)
### Inferences
- Realistic picture-book targets: Marilyn Baillie, a BBKT listing, Chocolate Lily (BC creators only), and IPPY.

## 8. Associations

### Takeaway
Self-published authors join CANSCAIP as Friends (CAD 50) and SCBWI as "Full" members (not PAL). They can become full TWUC members after peer review (CAD 100, then 205/yr), which also opens BC and Ontario grants and the readings program.

### Cited Findings
- **TWUC:**
  - You need 6 points, at least 3 of them from publishing. A sole-author book counts for 6.
  - A self-published book is reviewed for "professionalism and commercial intent", which can take up to 12 weeks.
  - Submit the full PDF or EPUB, the ISBN, sales and marketing information, and the editors, designers and publicists you used.
  - Fees: $100 application (credited or refunded), $100 for the first year, then $205/yr ($50 for Indigenous members).
  - Benefits include contract help, mentorship microgrants, paid readings, and ALLi resources.

## Gotchas and open questions

### Gotchas
- **Awards:**
  - The TD Canadian Children's Literature Award is gone.
  - Forest of Reading bars self-published, print-on-demand and "Amazon published" books.
  - Several venues ban AI-generated content: Hackmatack, CCBN, and Forest of Reading (per a snippet).
- **Grants:**
  - OAC excludes picture books.
  - AFA excludes self-published and print-on-demand projects.
  - BC requires two works with 400+ sales each, or TWUC membership.
- **Amazon:**
  - Countdown Deals are unavailable on Amazon.ca, cover ebooks only, and require KDP Select exclusivity.
  - Sponsored Brands needs 3 titles.
  - Ads bill per marketplace, with a $50 pre-authorization hold.
  - Gift cards void reviews.
- **CCBC shipping:** If you print in the US, you must prepay shipping and duty; COD is refused.
- **PLR:** You need an ISBN and at least 24 pages. Activity and colouring books are ineligible. The registration window is short (15 Feb–1 May in 2025).
- **Access Copyright:** Sign up by December 31 of the year before. The 60% share requires a claim filed April 1–May 31.
- **Missed 2026 deadlines:** OAC (Sep 10), BCAC (Oct 8), Hackmatack (Oct 15).
- **Next deadlines:** IPPY (2026-12-16), Chocolate Lily (2027-01-31), NGIBA (2027-02-12).
- **TWUC NPR:** excludes school visits, and 2026 funding is unconfirmed.
- **TPL:** won't prepay. **CASL:** implied consent expires.
- **Old sources:** MBR (2014) and CASL (2019).
