# Children's Book Market Demand: What Sells for Amazon Self-Publishers and in Canada (as of 2026-11-24)

Legend: **[SOLID]** = Circana BookScan, AAP, BookNet, BTLF/Gaspard, StatCan, KDP help pages. **[EST]** = market-research firms, vendors, self-reported claims. **[UNVERIFIED]** = search snippet only (page not opened or blocked). Dates = source publication dates. The search budget ran out before the UK Nielsen, K-lytics and Elf on the Shelf checks.

## 1. Market size and recent trend (US, Canada, global)

### Takeaway
US children's print (excluding YA) is about a third of all US print units. It is the only segment growing in 2025–2026: fiction +1.6% and nonfiction +3.6% in 2025, and fiction about +5% so far in 2026. YA is collapsing against a tough comparison year. In Canada, juvenile+YA is 39% of print (44% in French Canada) in a market of about 48M units / C$1.15B.

## 2. Segment trends 2023–2026

### Takeaway
- **Growing:** infant/baby (led by Bluey and Ms. Rachel), activity/coloring (+18%), educational juvenile nonfiction, series-driven kids' graphic novels, and backlist board-book classics.
- **Flat:** picture-book-age "young readers," the largest segment.
- **Shrinking:** middle grade (since 2023) and YA (a 2026 collapse against a tough comparison).

### Cited Findings
- **Infant:** +11% in H1 2025 and +13% in Q3 2025, the top age segment, credited to Bluey and Ms. Rachel — [infoDOCKET Jul 17 2025](https://www.infodocket.com/2025/07/17/new-data-from-circana-bookscan-u-s-book-market-holds-steady-in-the-first-half-of-2025-results-and-highlights/); [infoDOCKET Oct 27 2025](https://www.infodocket.com/2025/10/27/circana-bookscan-reports-u-s-book-market-sales-hold-steady-in-q3/).
- **Young readers (picture-book age):** flat in Q3 2025 and "the largest segment by sales volume" — [infoDOCKET Oct 27 2025](https://www.infodocket.com/2025/10/27/circana-bookscan-reports-u-s-book-market-sales-hold-steady-in-q3/).
- **Board books:**
  - AAP children's "special bindings" were $89.9M in H1 2025, −1.5% — [Shelf Awareness, Sep 22 2025](https://www.shelf-awareness.com/theshelf/2025-09-22/aap:_sales_in_first_half_of_2025_down_1.7_to_$6.3_billion.html).
  - Board books still dominate BookScan's 2025 lists: Very Hungry Caterpillar 565,688; Brown Bear 516,043; Goodnight Moon 363,673; Rainbow Fish 357,861; Ms. Rachel's 100 First Words 348,361; Guess How Much I Love You 322,254; Grumpy Monkey 228,195 — [PW, Feb 10 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/childrens/childrens-industry-news/article/99672-2025-children-s-bestsellers-buzzy-series-additions-graphic-novel-retellings-and-beloved-picture-books.html).
- **Backlist dominance:** about 215 children's titles sold 100K+ in 2025. Only about 32 were frontlist; about 183 were backlist (same source; the count is approximate).
- **Middle grade:** −10% in 2023 (through Sept); −1M units in H1 2025; −2% in Q3 2025, "most challenged" — [Circana 2023](https://www.circana.com/post/decline-in-children-s-book-sales-highlights-middle-grade-reading-dilemma-circana-reports); [infoDOCKET Oct 27 2025](https://www.infodocket.com/2025/10/27/circana-bookscan-reports-u-s-book-market-sales-hold-steady-in-q3/).
- **YA:**
  - 2025 fiction −1.8% even with Sunrise on the Reaping selling 2,000,856 copies — [PW Jan 9 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/bookselling/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html).
  - 2026: −28.7% in Q1, −25.6% in H1, −20.7% over nine months. Only supernatural/horror grew in H1 — [PW Jul 9 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100790-print-book-sales-flat-in-first-half-of-2026.html); [PW Oct 13 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/101512-print-sales-stay-flat-through-september.html).
- **Kids' graphic novels:**
  - Dog Man holds 13 of the top 20 kids' GN slots for 2025. Warriors (HarperAlley) took 3 slots after Graphix held all 20 in 2024 — [ICv2, Jan 21 2026](https://icv2.com/articles/markets/view/61451/full-year-2025-circana-bookscan-top-20-adult-kids-graphic-novels).
  - All graphic novels: +9.2% to ~25.9M units in 2025 — [Comics Beat, Jan 9 2026](https://www.comicsbeat.com/graphic-novel-sales-rose-9-2-in-2025).
- **Activity/coloring** — [PW, Aug 14 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/101056-hot-activity-book-sector-offers-screen-free-fun.html) (Circana data):
  - 16.7M units in 2025 (+18%), vs +3% for children's overall, and +18% through May 23 2026.
  - Coco Wyo accounts for "a quarter of all coloring books sold in the U.S. this year."
  - Licenses cited: KPop Demon Hunters, Minecraft, Paw Patrol, Super Mario, Ms. Rachel, Pokémon, Star Wars. Publishers credit screen-time worries.
  - Games/activities/hobbies, the largest kids' nonfiction subgenre, rose 18.4% in H1 2026 — [PW Jul 9 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100790-print-book-sales-flat-in-first-half-of-2026.html).
- **Educational nonfiction:** activity books plus math, language arts and spelling drove juvenile nonfiction in H1 2025 — [infoDOCKET Jul 17 2025](https://www.infodocket.com/2025/07/17/new-data-from-circana-bookscan-u-s-book-market-holds-steady-in-the-first-half-of-2025-results-and-highlights/).
- **Licensed (Bluey, 2025):** Let's Play Games! 171,010; Where's Bluey? 164,696; 5-Minute Stories 151,990; Sleepytime 123,060 — [PW Feb 10 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/childrens/childrens-industry-news/article/99672-2025-children-s-bestsellers-buzzy-series-additions-graphic-novel-retellings-and-beloved-picture-books.html).
- **Early readers:** Green Eggs and Ham was the #1 kids' title in Q1 2026, at ~160K — [PW Apr 2 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100067-first-quarter-print-sales-fell-3-1.html).

### Inferences
- The growth pockets (licenses, activity/coloring dominated by Coco Wyo, graphic-novel franchises) are hard for a debut indie to enter.
- The largest addressable segment, picture books for young readers, is flat and dominated by decades-old backlist. A new picture book wins on a specific use or niche, not on general appeal.
- Middle grade and YA are poor bets for a first-time indie in 2026.

### Gaps
- No separate unit trends for picture books, chapter books or early readers, and no hardcover board-book unit trend.

## 3. Format: hardcover vs paperback, prices, why hardcover

### Takeaway
In US children's/YA dollars, paperback slightly outsells hardcover: about 48% vs 38% of H1 2025 format revenue. In 2025 hardcover slipped (−0.8%) while paperback grew (+2.1%). At the top of the charts, though, kids' bestsellers are mostly hardcover or board book.

### Cited Findings
- **AAP H1 2025, children's/YA:** paperback $477.4M (+1.3%), hardcover $381.7M (−0.3%), special bindings $89.9M (−1.5%), e-books $50.3M (+5.7%) — [Shelf Awareness, Sep 22 2025](https://www.shelf-awareness.com/theshelf/2025-09-22/aap:_sales_in_first_half_of_2025_down_1.7_to_$6.3_billion.html) [SOLID].
- **AAP full-year 2025, children's/YA:** trade paperback +2.1% ("best print performer"), hardcover −0.8%, digital audio +6.1%, e-book +1.1%. Digital = 7.7% of children's/YA sales — [PW Feb 10 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99667-trade-sales-were-mixed-in-2025-religion-sales-up.html) [SOLID].
- **All trade, 2025:** hardbacks $8.0B (+3.9%), paperbacks $7.8B (+2.0%) — [AAP](https://publishers.org/news/aap-statshot-annual-report-publishing-revenues-totaled-33-4-billion-for-calendar-year-2025/).
- **BookScan 100K+ sellers, 2025:** frontlist 21 HC vs 11 PB; backlist ~129 HC vs 54 PB. PW's HC lists include board books and Dog Man — [PW Feb 10 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/childrens/childrens-industry-news/article/99672-2025-children-s-bestsellers-buzzy-series-additions-graphic-novel-retellings-and-beloved-picture-books.html).
- **Prices:**
  - Paperback picture books are mostly $9.99–$14.99 — [Lychee Press, Sep 2026](https://lycheepress.com/pages/kdp-breakeven-profit-calculator) [EST, vendor].
  - Hardcover picture books list at about $16.77–$19.99 on 2025 lists — [B&N](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/blog/the-best-picture-books-of-2025); [Bookshop.org](https://bookshop.org/lists/best-picture-books-of-2025-story-time) [UNVERIFIED].
  - Jacketed HC $17.99 vs trade PB $7.99–$9.99 — [48hrbooks](https://www.48hrbooks.com/publishing-resources/blog/84/how-to-price-your-self-published-book) [UNVERIFIED].
- **Why hardcover:** vendors cite durability, collectibility and library preference for hardcover, with paperbacks used for bulk school/library buys — [Imprint Digital](https://imprintdigital.com/book-printing-services/hardback-vs-paperback) [UNVERIFIED, vendor].
- **Libraries:** juvenile books are 51% of Canadian library circulations (2025) — [BookNet press release](https://www.booknetcanada.ca/press-room/2026/3/31/sales-of-books-by-canadian-authors-and-canadian-publishers-up-in-2025).
- **KDP royalty:** 60% at list ≥ US$9.99 or ≥ CA$13.99, 50% below that, minus printing cost. The same tiers apply to paperback and hardcover — [KDP paperback royalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330); [KDP hardcover royalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS) [SOLID].

### Inferences
- Hardcover is the gift, library and classic format; paperback carries volume in YA, middle grade and graphic novels.
- For an Amazon gift picture book, a hardcover at about $17.99–$19.99 matches trade norms. A $9.99–$14.99 paperback is the price-competitive companion, and offering both is the norm.
- Digital is under 8% of kids' revenue, so print is the product.

### Gaps
- No unit-based hardcover/paperback share for juvenile.
- No Canadian format split (BookNet's full report is paywalled).
- No authoritative survey of picture-book prices.

## 4. Demand on Amazon: categories, topics, validation

### Takeaway
Amazon's own bestseller pages were blocked (503), so there is no first-party Amazon category data here. BookScan shows steady demand for bedtime, love/gift, milestone, holiday and faith titles. Indie successes cluster in social-emotional learning (SEL) and emotions series. Sales rank (BSR) tools give estimates only.

### Cited Findings
- **Evergreen sellers (2025 units):** bedtime: Goodnight Moon (board book) 363,673. Love/gift: I Love You to the Moon and Back 478,176; Guess How Much I Love You (BB) 322,254; Love You Forever (BB, Canadian Robert Munsch) 215,222. Milestone: Oh, the Places You'll Go! 564,047. Faith: The Action Bible ~478K; The Beginner's Bible >331K — [PW Jan 9 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/bookselling/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html); [PW Feb 10 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/childrens/childrens-industry-news/article/99672-2025-children-s-bestsellers-buzzy-series-additions-graphic-novel-retellings-and-beloved-picture-books.html).
- **Holiday titles over 100K (2025):** Little Blue Truck's Christmas 168,388; Halloween 159,506; Valentine 135,696; Grinch 132,958; How to Catch Santa Claus 114,701; Elf on the Shelf 113,000; Bluey: Hooray, It's Easter! 111,163; How to Catch the Easter Bunny 100,211 — [PW Feb 10 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/childrens/childrens-industry-news/article/99672-2025-children-s-bestsellers-buzzy-series-additions-graphic-novel-retellings-and-beloved-picture-books.html).
- **SEL:** both flagship indie series are SEL. Ninja Life Hacks has 99 "emotion-exploring characters" — [405 Magazine](https://www.405magazine.com/her-2024-catalyst-mary-nhin/). My Dragon Books covers anger, lying, anxiety, autism, grief and bullying — [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/series/234506-my-dragon-books).
- **Vendor niche lists (2026):** first day of school, separation anxiety, new sibling, bedtime fear, potty training, big feelings, routines. "Potty Training Books for Boys" and "Bedtime Stories About Big Feelings" are rated "medium competition" — [kdpbuilder](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/best-childrens-book-niches) [UNVERIFIED, vendor].
- **Niches flooded with AI:** kids' nonfiction biographies, with 517 suspected AI titles — [Indicator, Jun 11 2025](https://indicator.media/p/amazon-is-selling-hundreds-of-seemingly-ai-generated-biographies-for-children-d536). Board books and nursery rhymes with no named author — [Mother Jones, Nov 26 2025](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2025/11/ai-childrens-books/).
- **Checking demand with BSR:**
  - Rough guide: BSR under 10,000 = strong daily sales; under 100,000 = consistent; over 500,000 = infrequent.
  - Each format has its own BSR. The estimates come from Publisher Rocket data, and Amazon publishes no sales figures.
  - Source: [Kindlepreneur calculator](https://kindlepreneur.com/amazon-kdp-sales-rank-calculator/) [EST].
- **Categories:** KDP may change a book's categories "at any time to protect the customer experience" — [KDP Content Guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390).

### Inferences
- For the reader, the best debut is a picture book that solves a specific parent problem (bedtime, big feelings, new sibling, first day of school, potty, grief) or a seasonal gift title, planned as book 1 of a series.
- To validate, check the hardcover and paperback BSRs of the top ~20 results for the exact keyword. If most rank above 500K, demand is thin.
- Avoid AI-flooded niches: generic biographies, ABC books, and nursery-rhyme board books.

### Gaps
- No Amazon category rankings, K-lytics data, or demand data for personalized, divorce or neurodiversity books.

## 5. Indie success case studies

### Takeaway
Both flagship indie SEL series won through volume (40–300+ titles), a recurring character brand, Amazon-first paperback distribution, merchandise, and educator/counselor channels. Sales figures are self-reported and inconsistent.

### Cited Findings
**Mary Nhin, Ninja Life Hacks (Grow Grit Press):**
- **Aug 25 2021:** 64 titles in under 2 years; "surpassed the One Million book threshold"; sold as Amazon paperbacks. Licensees (Weldon Owen, Fashion Angels, Fun World) for activity books, board books, conversation cards, classroom decor, costumes; brick-and-mortar retail next — [Total Licensing](https://www.totallicensing.com/ninja-life-hacks-surpasses-1m-books/).
- **Sep 30 2024:** 300 books, 99 characters, plus classroom resources. Nhin: "over two million dollars in sales, but I lost over $200,000" (said of her "first school year in business"; the context is ambiguous) — [405 Magazine](https://www.405magazine.com/her-2024-catalyst-mary-nhin/).
- **Unverified claims:**
  - 2M and 3M copies sold — [Amazon author page](https://www.amazon.com/Mary-Nhin/e/B07PNM8N1V) [UNVERIFIED, 503].
  - "More than 1 million books by mastering the Amazon algorithms and optimization, and cross promoting" — [License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/brand-licensing-europe/childrens-author-mary-nhin-cracks-amazon-code-childrens-literary-properties) [UNVERIFIED, 403].
  - 8M copies — [Edmond Outlook](https://www.edmondoutlook.com/mary-nhins-ninja-life-hacks/) [UNVERIFIED, implausible].
  - Target sells hardcover editions [UNVERIFIED snippet].

**Steve Herman, My Dragon Books:**
- **Goodreads:** 40 primary / 43 total works. Book 1 is Potty Train Your Dragon (2017). The most-rated is Train Your Angry Dragon, with 284 ratings — [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/series/234506-my-dragon-books).
- **Own site:** "Hundreds of thousands of parents, teachers, school counselors, and therapists have used My Dragon Books." Bulk discount up to 20%. Plush, T-shirts, stickers, prints, activity books. Spin-off series My Unicorn Books and Dinosaur and Friends — [mydragonbooks.com](https://www.mydragonbooks.com/pages/about).
- **Imprint:** DG Books Publishing. Self-published kids' books with Middletown, Delaware colophons "may indicate printing on demand at an Amazon facility" — [Cotsen/Princeton, Sep 19 2020](https://cotsen.blogs.princeton.edu/tag/steve-herman/).
- **Amazon snapshot:** 68 books in the series; individual titles at BSR #154,229 and #342,673 — [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Diggory-Listens-His-Body-Sensations/dp/1649161514) [UNVERIFIED snippet].
- No sales, pricing or ad-spend data found for either author.

**Other:**
- Canada's top book by a Canadian author in 2025 was self-published: Greta Podleski's Every Salad Ever (One Spoon Media). It is an adult cookbook — [PW Mar 31 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/international/international-book-news/article/100051-canadian-print-book-sales-rose-4-1-in-2025.html).

### Inferences
- The shared pattern:
  1. A recurring character, with title formulas keyed to a parent problem ("Train Your Angry Dragon," "Angry Ninja").
  2. Dozens of titles published fast.
  3. Amazon paperback first, plus box sets.
  4. A business-to-business channel: teachers, counselors, therapists, bulk orders.
  5. Merchandise and licensing.
- A single hardcover is not the model; a series is. Nhin's "$2M sales / $200K loss" suggests scale does not equal profit.

### Gaps
- Verified units, prices, ad spend and format splits for both authors. The brief's "Diggory Press" was not found; the sources show DG Books Publishing.

## 6. AI-generated children's books

### Takeaway
The AI flood is documented in specific niches: kids' nonfiction biographies and no-name board books. Amazon's response is internal disclosure plus a cap on new titles per day. The backlash is making indie booksellers wary of all self-published kids' books.

### Cited Findings
- **KDP policy:** authors must disclose "AI-generated content (text, images, or translations)" when publishing or republishing. AI-assisted work needs no disclosure, and substantial edits do not change AI-generated status. Amazon removes non-compliant books and bans content "typically disappointing to customers" — [KDP Content Guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390) [SOLID].
- **Title-creation limit:** introduced Sept 2023. PW reported three titles per day, and Amazon confirmed that to the Guardian. Exemptions are case by case, and low-content books are included — [ALLi, Sep 23 2023](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/self-publishing-news-kdp-ai-new-limits-on-titles/).
  - A vendor says three per day is "the maximum Amazon currently allows" (Jul 2026, unsourced) — [Rockefeller Publishing](https://rockefellerpublishing.com/blogs/ai-generated-childrens-books-are-flooding-amazon-and-real-authors-are-paying-the-price/) [EST].
  - The disclosure is not shown to shoppers [UNVERIFIED, vendor snippets].
- **Indicator (Jun 11 2025):** 517 children's nonfiction books, mostly biographies, appeared partly or fully AI-generated, mimicking Little People, Big Dreams and Who Was? Tell-tale signs: synthetic author photos and cover errors ("Genshis Khan"); some ran as "Sponsored" ads. Amazon removed 198 after Indicator shared its list — [Indicator](https://indicator.media/p/amazon-is-selling-hundreds-of-seemingly-ai-generated-biographies-for-children-d536).
- **Wired (via AI Weekly, Jul 29 2026):** grandparents are gifting AI-made books. Cory Doctorow: "garbage books that exist to suck up money from the inattentive" — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/amazons-ai-generated-childrens-books-flood-gift-piles). The Wired page itself was blocked.
- **Mother Jones (Nov 26 2025):** board books with no named author; Bold Kids' "hundreds of books" contain factual errors. Great Lakes Independent Booksellers Association member stores refuse suspected AI books; Prologue Bookshop is warier of self-published and small-press titles; Wild Rumpus gets AI-written pitches from self-publishers. The US Copyright Office has ruled out copyright for machine-generated images. AI books are still "on the fringes of publishing" — [Mother Jones](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2025/11/ai-childrens-books/).
- **Vendor claim:** over 30% of new KDP titles in 2026 carry an AI disclosure, and "thousands" appear weekly. No source is given — [Rockefeller](https://rockefellerpublishing.com/blogs/ai-generated-childrens-books-are-flooding-amazon-and-real-authors-are-paying-the-price/) [EST/UNVERIFIED].

### Inferences
- Human illustration is now a selling point. Credit the illustrator prominently, show the process, and expect bookstores and libraries to scrutinize self-published titles.
- Because disclosure is internal, buyers cannot filter out AI books. Reviews and Look Inside quality signals therefore carry more weight.

### Gaps
- No independent measure of the AI share of new children's KDP titles, and no data on how much indie discoverability has suffered.

## 7. Canada: juvenile share, Canadian content, schools/libraries, Quebec, bilingual, Indigenous

### Takeaway
Canada leans more toward kids' books than the US: 39% of print, 44% in French Canada, and 51% of library circulation. "Buy Canadian" sentiment lifted the Canadian-authored share to 14%. But Quebec's jeunesse (children's) sales and its school/library purchasing fell sharply in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Shares:** juvenile & YA 39% of print in 2025; 44% of French-Canadian trade — [BookNet](https://www.booknetcanada.ca/blog/2026/3/31/the-canadian-book-market-2025); [PW](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/international/international-book-news/article/100051-canadian-print-book-sales-rose-4-1-in-2025.html).
- **Canadian content:** Canadian-authored books were 14% of print units, up from 12% in 2024 and 2023. Canadian-owned publishers were 6.2% (from 5.6%). PW ties the rise to "buy Canadian" campaigns after Trump's annexation threats — [BookNet press release](https://www.booknetcanada.ca/press-room/2026/3/31/sales-of-books-by-canadian-authors-and-canadian-publishers-up-in-2025); [PW](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/international/international-book-news/article/100051-canadian-print-book-sales-rose-4-1-in-2025.html).
- **Libraries and bestsellers:** juvenile books were 51% of circulations, and the top subject was Juvenile Fiction / Humorous Stories. Dav Pilkey was the #1 juvenile author for the sixth straight year — [BookNet press release](https://www.booknetcanada.ca/press-room/2026/3/31/sales-of-books-by-canadian-authors-and-canadian-publishers-up-in-2025).
- **Quebec (Gaspard 2025, released Mar 31 2026) [SOLID; the panel covers ~50–60% of the market, 489 stores]:**
  - Total market C$250.2M, −0.5% in value.
  - Jeunesse C$61.9M = 24.7% of the market (28.2% in comparable bookstores); −3.1% in value, −6.4% in units.
  - Institutional (school/library) jeunesse sales: −6.8% in value, −10.0% in units. The institutional channel's share of the market fell from 40.6% to 37.5%, while retail grew 4.6%.
  - Quebec-published books −3.8%, driven by −11.9% in institutional sales. Bande dessinée +1.6%, 77.2% of it from foreign publishers.
  - Sources: [ActuaLitté](https://actualitte.com/article/130291/international/livre-au-quebec-pourquoi-les-ventes-reculent-en-2025); [ALQ, Apr 1 2026](https://www.alq.qc.ca/news/Le-Bilan-Gaspard-2025-du-marche-du-livre-au-Quebec).
  - Le Devoir links the decline to education budget cuts — [Le Devoir](https://www.ledevoir.com/lire/968069/ventes-livres-quebecois-auraient-ete-minees-compressions-education) [UNVERIFIED, 403].
- **French immersion:** 11.6% of school enrolment in 2023/24, down from a 12.3% peak in 2020/21, falling almost everywhere. Minority official-language programs outside Quebec are 4.3% — [StatCan, Oct 28 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/251028/dq251028d-eng.htm).
- **Indigenous:**
  - Indigenous-focused main-subject sales +527% (2012–2021); Juvenile Fiction / Canada / Indigenous = 13% of those titles. Sales of 50 Indigenous publishers +1,083% over the decade; 38% of their titles are juvenile fiction. In 2021, 5% of buyers searched for books by Indigenous authors/illustrators — [BookNet, Jun 20 2022](https://www.booknetcanada.ca/blog/research/2022/6/20/indigenous-books-in-canada-subjects-sales-and-more) (dated).
  - Indigenous-book sales rose 22% from 2022 to 2023, with teacher-librarian demand following the 2015 TRC calls to action — [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/small-business/article-indigenous-publishers-preserve-stories-in-print-as-book-sales-rise/) [UNVERIFIED].
  - The UBC guide describes #OwnVoices pressure given "a history of misrepresentation, stereotyping, and well-meaning cultural appropriation" — [UBC guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/Indigenouschildrensliterature/publishing) [UNVERIFIED].
- **Bilingual EN/FR:** no market data found.

### Inferences
- A Canadian setting or authorship is a modest, rising advantage. It pays off mainly through Canadian indie bookstores, libraries and schools, not Amazon search.
- A French edition reaches a market where kids' books are 44% of trade. But Quebec's institutional channel is shrinking and favours local publishers, so a French edition sold only through Amazon KDP would reach few buyers.
- Non-Indigenous authors should not write Indigenous cultural stories; the market and educators want Indigenous-authored work.

### Gaps
- No Canadian juvenile growth rate, format split, Amazon/online share, Q4 share, or bilingual-book data.

## 8. Seasonality

### Takeaway
About 30% of US print units sell in Q4 (my estimate). In Canada the kids' holiday ramp starts ~10 weeks before Christmas, and juvenile holiday titles peak in November. Secondary peaks: Easter, Valentine's, Halloween, and graduation.

### Cited Findings
- **US Q4 share (my calculation):** 2025 full year = 762.4M units. The nine-month 2026 total of 531.3M was −0.1%, which implies about 531.8M for nine-month 2025. That leaves Q4 2025 ≈ 230.6M, about 30% of the year — computed from [PW Jan 9 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/bookselling/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html) and [PW Oct 13 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/101512-print-sales-stay-flat-through-september.html).
- **Canada timing:** juvenile/YA sales climb from around Thanksgiving, "a full 10 weeks before Christmas." Nonfiction starts ~7 weeks out and fiction ~5 weeks out (2007–2016 data) — [BookNet, Dec 19 2017](https://booknetcanada.ca/blog/2017/12/19/when-does-holiday-shopping-begin/).
- **Holiday peak month:** the share of Christmas & Advent titles in juvenile fiction rises from Aug/Sept, peaks in November (2.29%), and falls in December. The 2024 top title was the Bluey Advent Calendar Book Bundle — [BookNet, Dec 5 2024](https://booknetcanada.ca/blog/2024/12/05/unwrapping-holiday-sales-for-childrens-books/).
- **Easter:** an earlier Easter lifted Q1 2026 kids' sales — [PW Apr 2 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100067-first-quarter-print-sales-fell-3-1.html).
- **Graduation:** Oh, the Places You'll Go! sold ~482K in H1 2026 and "propped up the fiction category" — [PW Jul 9 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100790-print-book-sales-flat-in-first-half-of-2026.html).
- **Holiday 2026 outlook:** Visa expects Nov–Dec retail +4.5% and average gift spend of $777 (+5.6%). McLean recommends "series sets, retail exclusives, other value adds, bundles" — [PW Nov 17 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/101783-as-holiday-season-nears-publishers-look-for-growth-in-a-flat-book-market.html).

### Inferences
- For a holiday launch, the book should be live, reviewed and in stock by September. The ramp starts around Canadian Thanksgiving (Oct 12, 2026), about 10 weeks before Christmas.
- A holiday-only title earns one season a year. Evergreen topics sell year-round and still get the Q4 gift lift.

### Gaps
- No children's-specific Q4 share for the US or Canada.

## 9. Typical sales outcomes for self-published children's books

### Takeaway
Most self-published kids' books sell under 1,000 copies, a quarter sell under 100, and profits are small. The outliers are multi-book series with marketing skill.

### Cited Findings
- **Holt survey (2018; 78 authors; 52% picture books; 4% Canadian; dated, small sample)** — [Hannah Holt](https://hannahholt.com/blog/2018/5/17/self-publishing-childrens-books-a-look-at-the-numbers):
  - Copies: 25% sold under 100; most under 1,000 per project; top seller 35,000.
  - Money: median profit $600 (mean ~$2,900); up to 30% lost money; ~1% earn a living. Only 20% of self-published picture-book authors earned over $1,000 last year, vs 75% traditionally published.
  - Top-30% sellers: all but one invested upfront, 70% own brand, 74% multiple books; 67% of the bottom third had one book. Authors rating their marketing 4–5 sold ~2× as many.
  - Distribution: 77% print-on-demand; 59% in at least one library; none had wide bookstore distribution. 20% used vanity presses, none of them in the top half.
- **Vendor estimate (Sep 2026, unsourced):** median lifetime 250–500 copies; top 20% over 1,000; under 5% over 5,000 — [Lychee Press](https://lycheepress.com/pages/kdp-breakeven-profit-calculator) [EST].
- **Royalty per sale** for a 32-page, 8.5×8.5, premium-colour KDP paperback: $9.99 → $1.79, $12.99 → $3.59, $14.99 → $4.79. Printing is $4.20 for 24–40 pages — [Lychee Press](https://lycheepress.com/pages/kdp-breakeven-profit-calculator) [EST, using the KDP formula].

### Inferences
- 500 copies × ~$3.60 ≈ $1,800 in royalties, which is likely less than professional illustration costs (see production notes).
- The realistic plan for book 1 is 100–500 copies. The upside comes from books 2–5 in a series and from library, school and local channels.

### Gaps
- No survey after 2018 and no Canadian-specific outcome data.

## Gotchas
- **Amazon.ca is not a primary KDP hardcover marketplace.** A hardcover sells on Amazon.ca only if it is made available on Amazon.com. Ads can still run on .ca — [KDP Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B); [KDP Distribution Rights](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834280).
- **The 60% royalty threshold on Amazon.ca is CA$13.99**, not CA$9.99 — [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS).
- **KDP hardcovers reportedly need at least 75 pages and have no 8.5×8.5 square trim.** That conflicts with a standard 32-page picture book. This is a vendor claim to check on KDP's spec pages — [Lychee Press](https://lycheepress.com/pages/kdp-breakeven-profit-calculator). Expanded Distribution is reportedly unavailable for hardcovers [UNVERIFIED].
- **Circana restates baselines:** 2024 was reported as 782.7M units, yet 2025's 762.4M is "+0.3%". Don't mix totals from different releases.
- **PW lists disagree:** Big Jim Believes is 767,342 in one list and 793,474 in another (different cut-off weeks). PW's "HC" lists include board books.
- **AAP's two children's/YA totals are not comparable:** $2.47B (1,324 reporting publishers) vs $6.4B (modelled annual).
- **BookNet rounding:** both 39% and "40%" appear; its blog wrongly calls 39% "the majority."
- **Quebec's jeunesse share depends on scope:** 24.7% of the overall table vs 28.2% in comparable bookstores. The panel covers only 50–60% of the market.
- **The YA collapse in 2026 is mostly a comparison effect** against Sunrise on the Reaping (~2M copies in 2025). The Q1 2026 kids' gain was partly Easter timing.
- **Indie sales claims are self-reported and inconsistent** (Nhin: 1M, 2M, 3M, 8M).
- **Activity/coloring growth is real but concentrated.** Coco Wyo alone accounts for ~25% of US coloring books.
