# Distribution channels for a self-published Canadian children's hardcover (as of 2026-11-24)

Method: the Indigo, IngramSpark (IS) and Kobo Writing Life (KWL) help pages blocked direct fetches (HTTP 403), so I read them through each help centre's public Zendesk API, which returns the same text. Citations use the canonical page URLs. The search budget ran out partway through, and "unverified" means the item comes from a search snippet only. Amounts are CAD unless marked.

## 1. Amazon.ca vs Amazon.com (KDP, Ingram, FBA) and Amazon's share

### Takeaway
A standard 32-page picture book cannot be a KDP hardcover: KDP requires at least 75 pages. Even a longer KDP hardcover reaches Amazon.ca only by being enabled on Amazon.com, and it is excluded from Expanded Distribution. That leaves two realistic hardcover routes: IngramSpark, which prints in the US for Canadian orders, or an offset print run sold through Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA). BookNet does not publish an Amazon market share.

### Cited Findings
- **KDP hardcover specs:** 75–550 pages for every ink type; trim sizes only 5.5×8.5, 6×9, 6.14×9.21, 7×10 or 8.25×11 in, because custom trims are paperback-only (so no square picture-book sizes); case laminate binding; premium colour, no standard colour. A KDP premium-colour paperback allows 24–828 pages. — [KDP Print Options, undated](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180)
- **Amazon.ca and hardcovers:** "Amazon.ca isn't a primary marketplace for hardcover books… you'll need to make it available on Amazon.com." Amazon.ca is a primary marketplace for paperbacks. "Expanded Distribution is not available for hardcover books." — [KDP rights, undated](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834280)
- **Author copies:** paperback author copies come from Amazon.ca, but "Hardcover proof and author copies are not currently available for Amazon.ca… select Amazon.com." — [KDP copies, undated](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVEG4YA9G2T7N6DR)
- **KDP paperbacks on indigo.ca:** paperbacks in KDP Expanded Distribution are "supplied wholesale by Ingram" to indigo.ca and take "a few weeks" to appear on Ingram. — [Indigo, ed. 2026-04-22](https://indigohub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31928563883796-FAQ-for-Authors)
- **IngramSpark has no Canadian print facility:** Canadian orders "are shipped to our Canadian distribution partner from Ingram in the U.S.", and Canadian sales pay the same as US sales. — [IS pricing, ed. 2026-02-06](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5281108644621-Pricing-Your-Book-For-Ingram-s-Global-Distribution-Network)
- **Ingram's reach:** Ingram sends title data to 40,000+ bookstores, libraries and retailers and sells only to businesses. IngramSpark "does not actively sell or promote your book." — [IS distribution, ed. 2026-02-06](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5338716228365-Distribution-of-Print-Books)
- **Amazon.ca seller fees** (relevant to FBA): Individual plan $1.49 per item, or Professional plan $29.99 per month; books pay a 15% referral fee, with no minimum or closing fee listed. FBA fulfilment for standard-size items runs from $5.92 (first 100 g) to $10.32 (1.5–9 kg), and is $0.80 lower for items priced under $14. — [Amazon.ca, undated](https://sell.amazon.ca/pricing)
- **Amazon's share:** BookNet "does not disclose company-specific information". A 2022 Canadian Heritage report found online sales data "notoriously incomplete, in part because Amazon does not report its Canadian sales in any detail". — [Globe and Mail, 2023-09-15](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-indigo-difficulties-publishing-industry/). A Statista survey from December 2024 ranks Amazon first among store brands; no share figure was visible (unverified). — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/998497/book-purchases-by-store-brand-in-canada/)
- **BookNet 2025 survey** (n=1,979): 53% of book purchases were online and 47% in person. Print made up 76% (paperback 48%, hardcover 28%), ebooks 14% and audiobooks 7%. Juvenile titles were 9% of books bought, and 55% of gift recipients were under 18. — [BookNet, 2026-05-11](https://booknetcanada.ca/blog/2026/5/11/canadian-book-buyers-in-2025)

### Inferences
- Both hardcover routes serve Canadian Amazon buyers from the US, so delivery and pricing on Amazon.ca are probably weaker than for a KDP paperback. Only offset-printed stock sent into FBA sits in Canada.
- A KDP hardcover can never reach Indigo or Ingram's customers.

### Gaps
- How Ingram titles display on Amazon.ca, and explicit confirmation of the Ingram-to-Amazon feed.
- Duties charged on KDP hardcovers shipped to Canada.
- KDP's print royalty and print costs.
- Whether Amazon Advantage operates in Canada.

## 2. Indigo/Chapters/Coles

### Takeaway
Indigo buys only from distributors, except on consignment, which is arranged store by store at each manager's discretion. To be ordered for stores, a book must be fully returnable, carry a discount of at least 45%, and show a Canadian price on the cover. Indigo uses Ingram mainly for online orders, but it will consider stocking nearby stores with a returnable Ingram title at a 55% discount.

### Cited Findings
Source unless noted: [Indigo FAQ for Authors, created 2024-11-07, ed. 2026-04-22](https://indigohub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31928563883796-FAQ-for-Authors).
- **No direct buying except consignment:** "We work with distributors and publishers, and not directly with authors, except on Consignment." Consignment is available at all stores, but each store must be approached separately, so it is "mainly practical in stores near where you live". The store manager decides. Request the consignment package from newauthor@indigo.ca.
- **Website listing:** a book can be listed on indigo.ca if it is available at regular terms from a distributor Indigo works with ("Print on Demand from Ingram, for example"). Ingram POD titles may need a manual listing: email the ISBN and title.
- **Terms for store orders:** "fully returnable"; "regular discount (≥45%)"; "Canadian Price on the cover/jacket".
- **Ingram titles in stores:** "we mainly use Ingram as a supplier for online orders, as the terms generally don't make it feasible to carry stock in our physical stores. However, if your book is showing as Regular discount and Returnable, we can consider listing select stores." Indigo starts with stores near the author and adds more based on the marketing plan and demand.
- **Ingram settings:** set "55% Wholesale… for both the US and Canada markets". The CAD price should be above the USD price at current exchange rates, because Canadian retailers pay Ingram in USD.
- **Returns:** choose "Return Yes: Destroy". "Ingram charges anyone outside the US $20 USD per copy to deliver returns" (100 copies = USD 2,000), on top of the printing cost of returned copies.
- **Ebooks and events:** indigo.ca ebooks come from Kobo. Events are booked with the store manager. Indigo "will never solicit money directly from authors".
- **Becoming a direct vendor:** requires Indigo's commercial terms (NewPublisher@indigo.ca), EDI capability and an inventory file. — [Indigo vendors, ed. 2024-11-12](https://indigohub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31934683210644-FAQ-for-Book-Vendors)
- **Requirements for publishers:** an ISBN and EAN barcode on the back cover. Buyers also weigh a printed Canadian price and the title on the spine, though square spines are acceptable for children's books. Local-interest titles must be unique, promoted, competitively priced, professionally packaged and fully returnable. Titles must be listed on CataList. — [Indigo publishers, ed. 2024-11-12](https://indigohub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31934865393556-FAQ-for-Book-Publishers)

### Inferences
- Likely sequence: an indigo.ca listing via Ingram, then consignment in 2–5 nearby stores, then a pitch to the regional buyer once there is sales evidence.
- A jacketed hardcover needs the CAD price printed on the jacket.

### Gaps
- Indigo's consignment split (it is in the package, which I did not obtain).
- Quill & Quire coverage (search budget exhausted).

## 3. Canadian indies (CIBA, Bookmanager), US indies and Bookshop.org

### Takeaway
Local consignment is the way into Canadian indie bookstores. One verified 2026 policy pays the author 60% of the retail price, but the same store now rejects AI-assisted books and deprioritises Amazon-printed ones. Stores that order through wholesalers, including Barnes & Noble, expect a 53–55% discount and returnability.

### Cited Findings
- **Words Worth Books (Waterloo, ON):** [Words Worth](https://words-worth-books.squarespace.com/book-consignment)
  - The author gets 60% of the retail price and the store keeps 40%.
  - Terms: 3–5 copies for 3 months (extendable), paid at the end of the term; unsold copies are held 30 days, then donated.
  - Requirements: local authors only; a signed agreement, an invoice and a marketing plan; no intake in November or December.
  - Notice dated 2026-09-09: no new consignments for the rest of 2026; from January, priority goes to books "not… printed or published by Amazon"; "Absolutely no AI-generated or AI-assisted books."
- **CIBA:** CIBA's "Guidelines for Self-Published Authors" (PDF, February 2024) returned HTTP 403, so its contents are unverified. — [CIBA](https://www.indiebookstores.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/CIBA-Guidelines-for-Self-Published-Authors.pdf)
- **Booksellers.ca** launched in February 2026, extending Québec's leslibraires.ca to English Canada. Buyers choose which indie gets the sale; it sells print, ebooks and audio, with free shipping over $49. — [BCBusiness, 2026-03-31](https://bcbusiness.ca/business/bc-bookstores-booksellers-ca-canada-platform/)
- **IngramSpark returns:** "Retailers and bookstores typically will not order a book unless it is returnable."
  - Yes-Deliver: wholesale cost plus USD 3 per book for US addresses, or USD 20 for non-US addresses.
  - Yes-Destroy: wholesale cost only; not available in Global Connect markets.
  - Non-returnable is also an option.
  - Source: [IS returns, ed. 2026-03-11](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5338718331277-Distribution-Returns-Information)
- **Discounts:** IngramSpark allows 40–55%. It suggests "53% to 55%… in the US (includes Canada)" and 48–55% for the UK, EU and Australia. — [IS vs Lightning Source, ed. 2026-02-06](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5281059609101-Difference-between-IngramSpark-and-Lightning-Source)
- **Barnes & Noble:** Ingram POD titles are automatically on bn.com and available by special order. To be stocked in stores, a book needs a 53–55% discount and returnability, kept "for the life of the book". — [IS B&N, ed. 2026-02-12](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5338717535245-Barnes-Noble-Requirements)
- **IngramSpark fees:** setup is free, revisions have been free since 2026-02-01, and there is a 1.875% market access fee. — [IS changes, ed. 2026-02-12](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/41657495062029-Changes-in-Print-Cost-Market-Access-Fee-and-More)
- **IngramSpark payment:** you receive the list price minus the discount, the print cost and the 1.875% fee, paid 90 days after the month of sale. Returns can arrive "at any time and for any reason". — [IS pricing](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5281108644621-Pricing-Your-Book-For-Ingram-s-Global-Distribution-Network)
- **IngramSpark print costs** (unverified; from a pricing PDF dated "Effective February 1st, 2026", opened from a cached copy whose URL was not captured), for a hardcover without jacket:
  - Premium colour: USD 6.51 + 0.0752 per page (small trim) or USD 6.69 + 0.1003 per page (large trim).
  - Standard colour: USD 6.38 + 0.0359 per page (small) or USD 6.55 + 0.0572 per page (large).
  - Hardcovers can run 18–840 pages.
- **Bookshop.org** is a Draft2Digital ebook partner. — [D2D, © 2026](https://draft2digital.com/partners). I found no Canadian Bookshop.org (unverified).

### Inferences
- With IngramSpark, a Canadian author should choose Destroy for returns.
- Locally, consignment (60% of retail) pays far better than a 55% wholesale discount on a print-on-demand hardcover (see the shortlist).

### Gaps
- What the CIBA guidelines actually say.
- Bookmanager: its homepage returned only a title.
- Consignment norms at other stores.
- Whether Ingram supplies Bookshop.org's print orders.

## 4. Libraries

### Takeaway
Canadian public libraries buy through wholesalers. Library Bound lists self-published titles for free and passes orders on to the author. The Public Lending Right (PLR) program pays authors whose books are held in sampled libraries. In the US, Baker & Taylor wound down at the end of 2025, leaving Ingram dominant. Library ebooks go through KWL (to OverDrive) or Draft2Digital (to OverDrive, hoopla and others).

### Cited Findings
- **Library Bound:** [LBI, © 2026](https://librarybound.com/contact/self-published-section)
  - To list a title, email nicola.rose@librarybound.com with the title, author, ISBN, Canadian list price, publication date, format, synopsis, and ordering and remittance details.
  - LBI creates a record and forwards library orders "to you, or your distributor".
  - "We do not sell into the school market or to trade bookstores."
- **Other vendors (older sources):** a 2014 study found vendors "welcoming to self-published authors". It cites Library Bound and the "Small Press" page of Library Services Centre (LSC). — [Dalhousie, 2014](https://ojs.library.dal.ca/YAHS/article/view/3500/4895). For Whitehots and ULS I have only a 2012 snippet (unverified). — [CULC](https://culc.ca/2012/07/joint-acquisition-of-s-b-books)
- **Public Lending Right** (2026 criteria): [PLR](https://publiclendingright.ca/eligibility)
  - Eligibility: Canadian citizens or permanent residents; a book published within the last 5 years with a new 13-digit ISBN; children's books need at least 24 pages; audiobooks qualify.
  - Illustrators of 24–32-page picture books can claim up to 50% of the payment.
  - The title must be held by a library in that year's sample, and the minimum payment is $50.
  - Unverified: payment range of $50–4,500, the registration window, and explicit eligibility of self-published books.
- **Canadian library use:** 35% of book buyers borrowed from a library in 2025. — [BookNet, 2026-05-11](https://booknetcanada.ca/blog/2026/5/11/canadian-book-buyers-in-2025). Libraries supplied 21% of ebooks acquired. Among ebook readers, 11% used OverDrive/Libby, 6% hoopla and 6% cloudLibrary. — [BookNet, 2026-07-14](https://www.booknetcanada.ca/scrolling-stories-ebook-use-in-canada-2025)
- **Baker & Taylor:** it wound down "by the end of 2025", affecting more than 4,000 institutional customers. Ingram and Brodart remain, and Follett Content has expanded into children's titles for public libraries. — [American Libraries, 2025-10-08](https://americanlibrariesmagazine.org/2025/10/08/baker-taylor-to-cease-operations/)
- **Where US libraries are moving:** Ingram is the "main beneficiary". Bookazine is adding library sales reps. Amazon Business Books for Libraries launched in June 2025 with 30–40% discounts on selected titles. — [PW, 2025-10-09](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20251013/98808-libraries-look-to-fill-the-gap-left-by-baker-taylor.html)
- **KWL to OverDrive:** opt in title by title and set a USD library price (higher than retail is recommended). The author earns 50% of it, with no aggregator fee. — [KWL, ed. 2026-09-18](https://kobowritinglife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059385651-OverDrive-Distribution). KWL audiobooks cannot go to libraries "at this time". — [KWL audio FAQ](https://kobowritinglife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058976312-FAQ-Audiobooks)
- **Draft2Digital:** library partners are OverDrive, cloudLibrary, BorrowBox, hoopla and Vivlio (added 2026). Amazon distribution is "by invitation only". — [D2D](https://draft2digital.com/partners)

### Inferences
- Minimum library setup: an Ingram-listed hardcover, a Library Bound listing, PLR registration, and library ebooks through KWL or D2D. KDP Select would block the library ebooks.

### Gaps
- Local-author donation programs at Canadian libraries.
- Library binding vs hardcover preferences.
- Current policies at Whitehots, ULS and LSC.
- How to get onto Follett and Mackin.
- PublishDrive.
- Routes for library audiobooks.

## 5. Schools

### Takeaway
School visits earn author-set fees plus book sales arranged in advance. Scholastic Canada reviews already-published books for its Book Clubs, but its pages are silent on self-published titles, and its book fairs are curated in-house.

### Cited Findings
- **CANSCAIP guidance:** [CANSCAIP, © 2025](https://canscaip.org/VisitsFAQ)
  - Presenters set their own fees, and many add GST/HST. Mileage is cited at $0.37/km, and hosts usually cover lodging beyond driving distance.
  - When books will be sold, teachers should send notice home beforehand.
  - The Writers' Union of Canada (TWUC) subsidises visits through the National Reading Program, Writers-in-the-Schools (Ontario and Northern Ontario) and BC/Yukon programs.
- **Example fees:** $350 + HST per presentation and $300 + GST per session (unverified). — [authorsbooking](https://authorsbooking.com/author/caroline/page/4/)
- **Scholastic Canada Book Clubs:** email a cover letter and a PDF of the published book to submissions@scholastic.ca; mail is not accepted. Reviews take up to 60 days, and only authors of books of interest get a reply. — [Scholastic, © 2026](https://www.scholastic.ca/aboutscholastic/submission-guidelines.php)
- **Scholastic Canada fairs:** a "carefully curated selection"; schools earn Scholastic Dollars; a $35 delivery fee applies. — [Fairs FAQ, c. 2025](https://www2.scholastic.ca/bookfairs/faq/)
- Library Bound does not sell to schools (see section 4).

### Inferences
- Visits are probably the highest-margin print sales, but the author needs stock on hand.
- Curriculum tie-ins have not been verified as a sales driver.

### Gaps
- How Canadian school libraries and school boards buy books.
- Whether Scholastic accepts self-published books.

## 6. Direct-to-consumer and Kickstarter

### Takeaway
Shopify with Lulu Direct automates print-on-demand hardcover fulfilment, and the author keeps the customer data. Etsy charges USD 0.20 per listing plus 6.5%. Kickstarter's publishing category is booming, but Kickstarter publishes no current success rate for children's books. Posting books to the US from Canada now means prepaying duties.

### Cited Findings
- **Lulu Direct:** orders are "fully automated", and Lulu prints and ships white-label to 150 countries. The author can "retain all your customer data". Hardcover is among 3,000+ options; the page lists no costs. — [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/sell/sell-on-your-site/shopify). The Shopify app is rated about 3.1/5 from about 43 reviews (unverified). — [Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/lulu-direct)
- **Etsy:** USD 0.20 per listing, plus a 6.5% fee on the order total; payment processing varies by country. — [Etsy, ed. 2024-12-05](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035902374-Etsy-Fee-Basics)
- **Kickstarter 2025:** the /help/stats page now redirects to a year-in-review post. Publishing raised more than USD 45M in 2025, second only to 2022, and several projects passed USD 1M. The post gives no success rates. — [Kickstarter, 2025-12-23](https://updates.kickstarter.com/a-year-in-review-2025-kickstarter-highlights/)
- **Earlier success rates:** publishing projects rose from about 30% to about 60% successful in data up to January 2021. Children's Books was once about 25% of the category. Across all categories, the rate was 41.02% (November 2023). — [Author Stack, 2024-02-07](https://theauthorstack.beehiiv.com/p/kickstarter-publishing-category-growth)
- **Anecdote:** one picture-book campaign was 60% funded after a week. The author's advice: "include shipping costs" in the goal. — [Jane Friedman, 2016](https://janefriedman.com/using-kickstarter-childrens)
- **Canada Post to the US:** [Canada Post, eff. 2025-11-11](https://origin-www.canadapost.ca/cpc/en/support/campaign/shipping-to-us-duty-updates/faq.page)
  - Since 2025-08-29, "All postal shipments valued under US$800 to the U.S. will require prepaid duties."
  - Books (customs Chapter 49) "still qualify for exemptions related to informational materials".
  - Zonos, the duty processor, charges $3.99 (converted) plus 10% on its Prepay app, or $1.99 plus 10% of duties with a Verified Account.
  - A Declaration ID and country of origin are required, and letter-post cannot be used for books sold to the recipient.

### Inferences
- Printing in the US (print-on-demand dropship) sidesteps postal duties for US buyers. Signed or personalized copies have to ship from Canada.

### Gaps
- Kickstarter's fees (the page returned 403).
- Success rates and typical goals for children's book campaigns.
- Etsy's processing fee in Canada and its policy on books.
- Craft-fair norms and personalized-edition services.

## 7. Ebooks

### Takeaway
Picture-book ebooks need to be fixed-layout files (EPUB3, or KPF for Kindle). Kobo is growing in Canada and requires no exclusivity. KDP Select (whose subscription is now called Kindle+) requires digital exclusivity, which shuts out Kobo, Apple, Google and libraries. Children's books are only 2% of Canadian ebook purchases.

### Cited Findings
- **KDP fixed layout:** no MOBI files for fixed layout since 2025-03-18; upload EPUB or KPF. "Children's books with text pop-ups" are supported. — [KDP FXL](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GULSQMHU5MNH4EZM). Unverified: Kindle Kids' Book Creator was withdrawn in February 2025.
- **KDP royalties:** the 70% option is reduced by delivery costs based on file size (average USD 0.06); the 35% option has none. — [KDP royalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210)
- **KDP Select:** "make the digital format… available exclusively through KDP". Enrolled books go automatically into Kindle+, with Countdown Deals and free promotions. KDP Select author earnings were USD 71.1M in October 2026. — [KDP Select](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/select)
- **Kindle+:** Kindle Unlimited became Kindle+ in October 2026. It costs $11.99 a month in Canada, has no audiobooks in Canada, and pays per page read. — [iPhone in Canada, 2026-10-22](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/10/22/kindle-unlimited-kindle-plus-audiobooks-canada/)
- **Kobo Writing Life:**
  - Fixed layout suits "an illustrated children's book". — [KWL ePub, ed. 2026-09-18](https://kobowritinglife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058975812-What-is-an-ePub)
  - Royalty is 70% at CAD/USD 2.99 or more, and 45% below that. — [KWL earnings](https://kobowritinglife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058976032-What-will-my-earnings-be)
  - Kobo Plus covers Canada, the US, UK, Australia and other markets, with no exclusivity. — [Kobo Plus, ed. 2026-10-29](https://kobowritinglife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058975432-What-is-Kobo-Plus)
  - KWL titles also feed indigo.ca. — [Indigo](https://indigohub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31928563883796-FAQ-for-Authors)
- **Canadian ebook readers, 2025:** 31% used Kindle (down from 35%), 22% Google Books, 16% Kobo (up from 11%) and 14% Apple. Children's books were 2% of ebook purchases and 10% of ebooks read. — [BookNet, 2026-07-14](https://www.booknetcanada.ca/scrolling-stories-ebook-use-in-canada-2025)
- **Other stores:** Apple, Kobo and Google accept fixed-layout EPUB, per a 2020 snippet (unverified). — [Book Designer](https://www.thebookdesigner.com/going-against-the-flow-reflowable-vs-fixed-layout-ebooks/)

### Inferences
- A 32-page book earns very little in a pay-per-page subscription. Going wide (KWL first, then KDP without Select) suits Canadian picture books better.

### Gaps
- Fixed-layout rules at Apple, Google and D2D.
- Amazon.ca's delivery cost per MB.

## 8. Audiobooks

### Takeaway
ACX is open to Canadian residents, and KWL and Spotify for Authors are direct alternatives. For a picture book, audio is a low priority: it is 7% of Canadian purchases, and it loses the art.

### Cited Findings
- **ACX:** "open to residents of the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Ireland" who have a local tax number and bank account. — [KDP ACX](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201014330)
- **Kindle+ audio** is US-only for now. ACX titles must be exclusive to Audible, made under Pay-for-Production and have Whispersync on. AI virtual-voice audiobooks are included automatically when the ebook is in KDP Select. — [iPhone in Canada](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/10/22/kindle-unlimited-kindle-plus-audiobooks-canada/)
- **KWL audiobooks:** 45% royalty on individual sales at USD 2.99 or more, 32% on subscription-token sales. AI narration is accepted if labelled "Synthesized Voice". There is no library distribution, and the audio tool may have to be enabled on request. — [KWL audio FAQ, ed. 2026-09-18](https://kobowritinglife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058976312-FAQ-Audiobooks)
- **Spotify for Authors:** direct uploads to Spotify, plus optional sharing with Voices by INaudio to reach other retailers. It states no countries or royalty rates. — [Spotify, 2025-08-01](https://authors.spotify.com/blog/direct-audiobook-publishing). Unverified: Findaway Voices split into these two services in 2025. — [Reedsy](https://reedsy.com/blog/voices-by-inaudio-review/)
- **Market data:** audiobooks were 7% of 2025 purchases. — [BookNet](https://booknetcanada.ca/blog/2026/5/11/canadian-book-buyers-in-2025). PLR covers audiobooks with an ISBN. — [PLR](https://publiclendingright.ca/eligibility)

### Inferences
- A picture book read aloud runs only a few minutes and depends on its pictures, so human narration is unlikely to pay back. AI narration via KWL is the cheapest test.

### Gaps
- ACX royalty rates and minimum running time.
- Whether Spotify for Authors is available in Canada.

## 9. International sales, foreign and translation rights

### Takeaway
IngramSpark reaches the UK, EU and Australia (it suggests 48–55% discounts there) and handles the EU's General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) as manufacturer. Translation and foreign rights are sold publisher to publisher. Bologna (5–8 April 2027) admits authors as trade visitors. Canada Council translation grants go to publishers and organisations, not to authors.

### Cited Findings
- **IngramSpark reach:** it distributes to the UK, Australia, most EU countries and more. — [IS countries, ed. 2025-12-11](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/16684961523085-IngramSpark-Can-Provide-Print-Ebook-Distribution-Services-To-These-Countries). Australian pay has GST deducted. — [IS pricing](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5281108644621-Pricing-Your-Book-For-Ingram-s-Global-Distribution-Network). Global Connect covers Brazil, China, Germany, Italy, India, Japan, South Africa, South Korea, Spain, Turkey and Singapore, with no direct-to-customer shipping. — [GCP, ed. 2026-10-16](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5338732080781-Global-Connect-Program-GCP)

## Gotchas
- **No KDP hardcover under 75 pages**, and KDP hardcovers come in no square trims. — [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180). IngramSpark hardcovers start at 18 pages. — [IS trims, ed. 2026-05-13](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5341425168013-Available-Trim-Sizes-Bindings-for-Covers-Interior-Papers-for-Print-Books)
- **KDP hardcovers stay on Amazon:** no Expanded Distribution (so no Indigo, Ingram or libraries), Amazon.ca only through Amazon.com, and Canadian author copies ordered from Amazon.com. — [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834280), [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVEG4YA9G2T7N6DR)
- **IngramSpark ships Canadian orders from the US.** — [IS](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5281108644621-Pricing-Your-Book-For-Ingram-s-Global-Distribution-Network)
- **Price for Canada:** set the CAD price above the USD price, and print the CAD price on the cover or jacket. — [Indigo](https://indigohub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31928563883796-FAQ-for-Authors)
- **Returns:** choose Destroy, because delivered returns cost USD 20 per copy outside the US. — [IS](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5338718331277-Distribution-Returns-Information). Barnes & Noble bars later cutting the discount below 53% or making the book non-returnable. — [B&N](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/5338717535245-Barnes-Noble-Requirements)
- **IngramSpark charges a 1.875% fee and pays 90 days after the month of sale.** — [IS](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/41657495062029-Changes-in-Print-Cost-Market-Access-Fee-and-More)
- **Indie bookstores may reject AI-assisted books or deprioritise Amazon-printed ones.** — [Words Worth](https://words-worth-books.squarespace.com/book-consignment)
- **Mailing books to US buyers** requires prepaid duties through Zonos, a Declaration ID and parcel service rather than letter-post. — [Canada Post](https://origin-www.canadapost.ca/cpc/en/support/campaign/shipping-to-us-duty-updates/faq.page)
- **KDP Select exclusivity** shuts out Kobo, which is growing in Canada. — [KDP Select](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/select)
- **Library outlets:** Baker & Taylor is gone. — [ALA](https://americanlibrariesmagazine.org/2025/10/08/baker-taylor-to-cease-operations/). Library Bound skips schools and bookstores. — [LBI](https://librarybound.com/contact/self-published-section)
