# Canadian legal, tax and admin checklist: self-published children's hardcover sold on Amazon (as of 2026-11-24)

General information, not legal or tax advice. Federal rules first, with ON, QC and BC differences flagged. Label dates are each page's own update date. "Unverified" means search snippet only, or the page blocked fetching. The web-search budget ran out mid-task.

## 1. ISBNs and barcodes

### Takeaway
ISBN Canada (LAC) gives free ISBNs to self-publishers who are physically located in Canada. Publishers who publish only in French go to BAnQ. A new account can take up to 2 months. Every format needs its own ISBN. Since 2026 there is a human-authorship declaration, and ISBNs are capped for works that are primarily AI-generated.

### Cited Findings
- **Eligibility:**
  - ISBNs are free for print, ebook and audio. Self-publishers qualify if "physically located in Canada". Companies need their main place of business in Canada and at least 75% of staff in Canada. LAC may ask for proof of address or identity. — [LAC About ISBNs, 2026-10-09](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/about.html)
  - "Canadian publishers that publish in French only must contact" BAnQ. — [same](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/about.html)
- **AI policy:** You declare the book was "written by a human author". AI is allowed "only for limited support tasks". "ISBN Canada limits the quantity of ISBNs for works whose content is primarily generated by AI", and this covers illustration. The policy is under review. — [same](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/about.html)
- **Conditions:**
  - LAC may limit how many ISBNs you get. ISBNs cannot be sold or transferred.
  - You agree to legal deposit.
  - An ISBN gives no copyright protection.
  - "The Canadian ISBN Agency does not provide barcodes." — [same](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/about.html)
- **Processing time:** "It may take up to two months to process applications for new accounts." You create an account only once. — [LAC Apply, 2026-08-14](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/apply.html)
- **One ISBN per format:**
  - "Each format and edition of a publication must have its own ISBN." — [About ISBNs](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/about.html)
  - You self-assign ISBNs in the online logbook, per format. "Each e-book format (EPUB, MOBI, Kobo and PDF) also requires a separate ISBN." — [LAC instructions, 2025-07-30](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/instructions.html)
- **Amazon's free ISBN vs your own:** KDP's free ISBN is for paperback and hardcover only.
  - The imprint shows "Independently published".
  - "Free ISBNs from us can only be used on KDP."
  - With your own ISBN, the title, author and publisher must match the ISBN agency record, "If there's a mismatch, you won't be able to publish."
  - "Each format of your book (paperback vs hardcover) needs its own ISBN." — [KDP ISBN help, accessed Nov 2026](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834170)
- **Barcodes:**
  - KDP: "If you choose not to provide your own barcode, we'll automatically place one on your back cover." This is from the paperback page; I did not check the hardcover page. — [KDP cover help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020)
  - Kindlepreneur's free generator needs no account and outputs PDF, PNG or JPG. For the price add-on, 90000 means no price, 5xxxx is USD and 6xxxx is CAD (60699 = CAD$6.99). You must already own a valid ISBN-13. — [Kindlepreneur, undated](https://kindlepreneur.com/isbn-bar-code-generator/)
  - Damonza and isbnbarcode.org offer similar free tools (unverified). — [Damonza](https://damonza.com/isbn-barcode-creator/), [isbnbarcode.org](https://isbnbarcode.org/price-barcode)

### Inferences
- A hardcover, a paperback and one ebook need at least 3 ISBNs.
- An own LAC ISBN makes your imprint the publisher of record, and it can be used on both KDP and IngramSpark.
- A KDP free ISBN would force a second ISBN for an IngramSpark edition.

### Gaps
- How long ISBN assignment takes once you have an account. How KDP verifies LAC-issued ISBNs.

## 2. Legal deposit (LAC; BAnQ in Quebec)

### Takeaway
Within 7 days of making the book available, send LAC copies of each physical format at your own cost:
- 2 copies normally;
- 1 copy if fewer than 100 are produced, which includes print-on-demand from a master copy;
- nothing unless asked if fewer than 4 are produced.

Quebec publishers also deposit with BAnQ.

### Cited Findings
- **Federal duty:** A publisher making a publication available in Canada must, "at its own expense", give two copies to LAC. The default deadline is seven days after it is made available. Regulations set one-copy classes and on-request classes. — [LAC Act s.10, current to 2026-10-29](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-7.7/section-10.html)
- **Copy counts and print-on-demand:**
  - One copy for "publications of which fewer than 100 copies have been produced" (s.3(c)).
  - One copy for "publications produced on demand from a master copy of which fewer than 100 copies have been produced" (s.3(d)).
  - Fewer than four copies: deposit only on written request (s.4(a)).
  - Someone who only distributes is not a publisher. — [SOR/2006-337, current to 2026-10-29](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-337/FullText.html)
- **How to deposit:** 2 copies if 100+ produced, 1 if 4–99, "for all formats published (such as paperback, hardcover)". Use the monograph form and mail it "at your expense" to Legal Deposit, LAC, 550 de la Cité Blvd, Gatineau QC J8T 0A7. — [LAC physical deposit, 2025-08-21](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/legal-deposit/physical-publications.html)
- **Self-publishers and formats:** Self-publishers are covered. "Submit all physical formats", and digital versions too. The production cost of deposit copies is deductible, but their market value is not. — [LAC About legal deposit, 2025-07-30](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/legal-deposit/about.html)
- **Quebec (BAnQ):**
  - Two free copies within 7 days under s.20.1 of the Loi sur BAnQ. Unverified: from snippets, and LégisQuébec returned 403. — [LégisQuébec](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/B-1.2)
  - BAnQ's procedure guide (dated Aug 2015):
    - Declare in the BAnQ extranet, giving title, price and print run.
    - Send the printed form with the copies to BAnQ Dépôt légal, 2275 rue Holt, Montréal H2G 3H1.
    - Missing deposits trigger 3 email reminders, then a letter, a call and a notice, then BAnQ's legal department. — [BAnQ guide](https://prod-portail-origin.banq.qc.ca/sites/default/files/2022-09/guide_depot_legal_publications_analogiques.pdf)

### Inferences
- Print-on-demand hardcover plus paperback: likely 1 copy of each to LAC within 7 days of release.
- Quebec residents deposit with both LAC and BAnQ.

### Gaps
- BAnQ's current one-copy and print-on-demand rules. How print-on-demand "copies produced" are counted over time.

## 3. CIP (Cataloguing in Publication)

### Takeaway
Self-publishers are not eligible.

### Cited Findings
- LAC excludes "authors or contributors who publish their works at their own expense". It also excludes publishers that mainly publish one author, publishers that charge for publishing services, and publishers with no Canadian address. Apply at least 15 days ahead. French-language publishers in Quebec use BAnQ. — [LAC CIP, 2026-01-06](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/cataloguing-publication/applying.html)
- A 2017 report says self-published works were dropped from CIP (unverified). — [WordWorks](https://issuu.com/fbcw/docs/wordworks_summer_2017/16)

### Inferences
- Do not print a CIP data block.

## 4. Copyright, illustrator contracts, AI

### Takeaway
Copyright is automatic and lasts for life plus 70 years. CIPO registration is optional and unexamined, and costs $63 online. Canada has no work-made-for-hire rule for contractors: you need a written, signed assignment from the illustrator, plus a moral-rights waiver. No court has yet ruled on the copyright status of AI-generated work.

### Cited Findings
- **Term:** life of the author, plus the rest of the year of death, plus "70 years following the end of that calendar year". Amended by S.C. 2022, c.10. — [Copyright Act s.6, current to 2026-10-29](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-6.html)
- **Ownership and assignment:**
  - s.13(1): the author is the first owner.
  - s.13(3): the employer owns works employees make in the course of employment, absent agreement.
  - s.13(4): "no assignment or grant is valid unless it is in writing signed by the owner of the right" or the owner's agent.
  - s.13(7): an exclusive licence is a grant of an interest. — [s.13](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-13.html)
- **Moral rights (s.14.1):** rights to integrity, attribution (name or pseudonym) and anonymity. "Moral rights may not be assigned but may be waived in whole or in part." Assigning copyright does not by itself waive them, and a waiver can be invoked by people the owner or licensee authorizes. — [s.14.1](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-14.1.html)
- **Fees:**
  - $63 online, $81 otherwise; recording an assignment or licence costs $81. — [Copyright Regulations tariff, last amended 2024-01-01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-97-457/page-2.html)
  - CIPO: $63, plus $18 if not filed online. — [CIPO fees, 2024-07-22](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/node/33)
  - A June 2026 guide still says $50/$65 (unverified), which is outdated. — [lawyerinfo](https://lawyerinfo.ca/guides/money-taxes-ip/intellectual-property/how-to-legally-copyright-a-song-or-musical-composition-in-canada/)
- **Process:** File online, by mail or by fax. "CIPO does not verify or examine the claims" and needs no copy of the work. A certificate "is evidence that the copyright subsists". — [CIPO guide, 2024-10-15](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/guide-copyright)
- **No work-made-for-hire:**
  - "The concept of 'work made for hire' does not exist in Canadian law." Authorship stays "with the employee or contractor". — [Gowling WLG, 2013](https://gowlingwlg.com/en/insights-resources/articles/2013/canada-and-the-united-states-differences-in-copyr)
  - "Independent contractors retain copyright unless assigned." Waivers should spell out attribution, integrity, modifications and credit (Snow v. Eaton Centre). Assigned copyright reverts to the author's estate 25 years after death (s.14(1)). — [RGD/Entcounsel, 2026-03-19](https://rgd.ca/articles/2026-ask-the-expert-copyright-ip-for-designers-in-canada-part-2)
- **AI and the SURYAST case:**
  - CIPPIC filed on July 8, 2024 (Federal Court file T-1717-24) to expunge or rectify registration 1188619, which lists Ankit Sahni and RAGHAV AI as co-authors.
  - CIPPIC argues there is no originality (no human skill and judgment) and that an author must be human.
  - Status: "waiting for the Court to set a hearing date"; the latest update is dated July 31, 2025. — [CIPPIC](https://www.cippic.ca/our-work/cippic-v-sahni)
  - On Feb 2, 2026: a hearing requisition was filed on July 28, 2025, and "nothing appears to have happened since then". The same post notes that registration "involves no examination". — [Knopf, 2026-02-02](https://excesscopyright.blogspot.com/2026/02)

### Inferences
- The illustrator contract should include:
  - a signed assignment or exclusive licence (all media, worldwide, full term);
  - a moral-rights waiver (edits, cropping, adaptation) with credit terms;
  - an originality warranty and AI disclosure;
  - fee or royalty, revisions and a kill fee.
- A US "work made for hire" clause alone transfers nothing. Optionally register the assignment with CIPO ($81). Have a lawyer review it.
- AI-only images may be unprotectable; registration would not cure that.

### Gaps
- Any SURYAST developments after Feb 2026.
- The in-force date of the life+70 change.

## 5. Business structure, name registration, business number

### Takeaway
A sole proprietorship is simplest. Register the imprint name if it isn't your legal name. Get a CRA business number (BN) only for GST/HST or incorporation.

### Cited Findings
- **Business number:** Needed for a GST/HST, payroll or other CRA program account, or when you incorporate. An unincorporated business needs one "only when you register for program accounts". — [CRA BN, 2026-06-30](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/registering-your-business/you-need-a-business-number-a-program-account.html)
- **Ontario:**
  - Register if trading under a name other than your full name. Registration lasts 5 years, and you can renew up to 6 months before expiry. More than 60 days after expiry, you must register again with a new business number. Changes are due within 15 days. — [ON Business Names Act notice, effective 2025-02-01](https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca/en/dataset/6073def4-7470-47a9-bf13-4bc22cec9bda/resource/48361552-0263-4e5b-b4d6-43bbfac83fe1/download/on00227e.pdf)
  - Fee $60 (unverified, snippet). — [ON Business Registry](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-business-registry-all-services)
- **Quebec:**
  - Registration $41 ($61.50 priority). Annual update $41, which includes the annual fee. Late penalties: 50%, or 5% plus 1% per month. — [Registraire fees, 2026-08-31](https://www.quebec.ca/entreprises-et-travailleurs-autonomes/tarifs-registraire-entreprises/personne-physique-exploitant-une-entreprise-individuelle)
  - Register within 60 days if the name lacks your first and last names; other names must be in French (unverified). — [quebec.ca](https://www.quebec.ca/entreprises-et-travailleurs-autonomes/demarrer-entreprise/immatriculer-constituer-entreprise/immatriculation-entreprise/a-propos)
- **BC:** Nothing is needed under your own legal name. Otherwise, name request $30 ($100 priority), registration $40, within 56 days of name approval. — [gov.bc.ca, 2026-09-14](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/business/managing-a-business/permits-licences/businesses-incorporated-companies/proprietorships-partnerships)
- **Corporate tax rates:** Federal 15%, or 9% with the small business deduction. Ontario 2.2% (cut from 3.2% on July 1, 2026) and 11.5%. BC 2% and 12%. The small business limit is $500,000. Quebec is not listed. — [CRA corporate rates, 2026-10-21](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/corporation-tax-rates.html)
- **Royalties:** "generally" property income, unless connected to the recipient's business. — [Folio S4-F14-C1 ¶1.30–1.31](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-4-businesses/folio-14-income-artistic-endeavours/income-tax-folio-s4-f14-c1-artists-writers.html)

### Inferences
- A corporation means a T2 return, provincial filings, a written copyright assignment to the company (s.13(4)), entity tax forms for platforms (W-8BEN-E, unverified) and accountant fees.
- If royalties count as property income, the "specified investment business" rules could deny the 9% rate. For a one-book imprint, get an accountant's view before incorporating.

### Gaps
- Incorporation filing fees were not verified.

## 6. Income tax (CRA)

### Takeaway
Royalties are business income on form T2125 if you publish in a businesslike way with an intent to profit. CRA's current guidance is Folio S4-F14-C1, which replaced IT-504R2. Normal expenses are deductible, but home-office costs cannot create a loss. CRA gives no specific answer on whether illustration is a current or capital expense.

### Cited Findings
- **The folio:** first published Oct 9, 2020, when it "replaced and cancelled" earlier guidance; effective June 23, 2022. — [Folio S4-F14-C1](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-4-businesses/folio-14-income-artistic-endeavours/income-tax-folio-s4-f14-c1-artists-writers.html)
  - CRA's concordance maps IT-504R2 to this folio (unverified). — [concordance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/table-concordance-bulletin-folio.html)
- **Profit test:**
  - The test: "Is the activity... undertaken in pursuit of profit, or is it a personal endeavour?" It needs a "subjective intention to profit" plus businesslike conduct.
  - Factors include time spent, an agent or publisher, promotion, revenue, profit history, research spending, qualifications and memberships. "No particular factor... is more important."
  - A profit may never come in your lifetime, but the work must still be run like a business. — [Folio ¶1.18–1.26](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-4-businesses/folio-14-income-artistic-endeavours/income-tax-folio-s4-f14-c1-artists-writers.html)
- **Deductible expenses:** An expense must be incurred to earn income, not capital, not personal, and reasonable. Examples: legal and accounting fees, agent commissions, assistants, publicity, training. — [Folio ¶1.37–1.38](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-4-businesses/folio-14-income-artistic-endeavours/income-tax-folio-s4-f14-c1-artists-writers.html)
- **Nil inventory:** The s.10(6) nil-inventory election "is not available to an individual who is in the business of writing". — [Folio ¶1.35](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-4-businesses/folio-14-income-artistic-endeavours/income-tax-folio-s4-f14-c1-artists-writers.html)
- **Form T2125:** one per business. Gross sales include GST/HST, which is then deducted. — [T4002 2025 ch.2, 2026-04-16](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002/t4002-4.html)
- **Current vs capital:** "A capital expense generally gives a lasting benefit or advantage"; a current expense "usually recurs after a short period". — [T4002 ch.3](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002/t4002-5.html)
- **Advertising:** deductible, but periodical ads aimed at Canada can be limited to 50%, and ads on foreign broadcasters aimed mainly at Canada are not deductible. I did not fully extract the conditions. — [T4002 ch.3](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002/t4002-5.html)
- **Home office:** The space must be your principal place of business, or used only for business and regularly to meet clients. Allocate by area. It cannot create or increase a loss; the excess carries forward. — [CRA home office, 2026-08-31](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/completing-form-t2125/business-use-home-expenses.html)
- **Foreign currency:**
  - Bank of Canada annual average USD/CAD: 1.3698 (2024) and 1.3978 (2025). — [BoC](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/annual-average-exchange-rates/)
  - T4002's only explicit rule (for fishing) is the rate at the time of sale, or an average if sales occur through the year. — [T4002 ch.2](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002/t4002-4.html)

### Inferences
- Platform royalties from your own publishing go on T2125.
- Editing, ads, printing and author copies look current. Illustrations bought with a copyright assignment give a lasting benefit and could be capital (ask an accountant).
- Convert USD consistently, at the rate on the day received or the annual average (by analogy; confirm).
- Early losses need the profit test, so keep records.

### Gaps
- CRA's general foreign-currency rule (Folio S5-F4-C1) was not retrieved.
- Whether online ads fall under ss.19/19.1.
- How to claim a foreign tax credit if US tax is withheld.

## 7. GST/HST and QST

### Takeaway
- **Registration:** not required until worldwide taxable supplies exceed $30,000, and zero-rated royalties count. The threshold is unchanged.
- **Royalties:** royalties from an unregistered non-resident are generally zero-rated.
- **Direct sales:** a registrant charges tax on printed books sold directly, but in the five HST provinces collects only the 5% federal part.
- **Quebec:** books with an ISBN are zero-rated for QST (unverified).

### Cited Findings
- **Small supplier threshold:** $30,000 in a single quarter (you become a registrant on the supply that crosses), or over four consecutive quarters (you stop at the end of the following month). Register within 29 days. Counts worldwide taxable supplies, including zero-rated and associates' supplies. Voluntary registration is allowed. No 2025–26 change. — [CRA, 2026-06-16](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge)
- **Zero-rated royalties:** "Supplies of intangible personal property (IPP) made in Canada to non residents who are not registered" (under the normal regime) "are generally zero rated". Exceptions: individuals in Canada, IPP tied to Canadian real property, and IPP usable only in Canada. Copyrights may qualify. — [RC4022 Rev. 25](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4022/general-information-gst-hst-registrants.html)
- **Charging tax:** Registrants charge GST/HST on books delivered in Canada, and on rebate items "charge and collect only the federal part". — [CRA publications, 2026-06-25](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/suppliers-publications.html)
- **Point-of-sale rebate:**
  - Printed books qualify in NB, NL, NS, ON and PEI, but "not e-books, newspapers, magazines, catalogues, colouring books, agendas". — [RC4022](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4022/general-information-gst-hst-registrants.html)
  - Mechanics:
    - The supplier credits the provincial part and collects 5%.
    - Invoices show a rebate line or the federal part only.
    - On the return, use line 103, plus a line 107 adjustment if reporting gross.
    - Buyers can claim missed rebates on Form GST189 (reason 16) within 4 years.
    - A bundle qualifies only if at least 90% of its value is the book.
  - GI-065's rate table is outdated. — [GI-065, Aug 2014](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-065/point-sale-rebate-on-books.html)
- **Current rates:** ON 13%; NS 14% (since 2025-04-01); NB, NL and PEI 15%; QC 5% GST plus 9.975% QST; BC 5% GST plus 7% PST. — [CRA rates, 2025-04-01](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html)
- **QST:**
  - A printed book with an ISBN is zero-rated for QST, while GST still applies. The criteria are in bulletin TVQ 198.1-1/R3 (2014-06-27). Bundles that form a new product are taxable. Unverified: Revenu Québec returned 403. — [Revenu Québec](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/special-cases-gsthst-and-qst/printed-books/)
  - QST small supplier threshold also $30,000 (unverified). — [RQ](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/consumption-taxes/taxable-zero-rated-or-tax-exempt-goods-and-services/gst-and-qst/other-situations/details-concerning-small-suppliers/)

### Inferences
- Current provincial parts: ON 8%, NS 9%, NB, NL and PEI 10%. A registered seller therefore effectively charges 5% on printed books in every province; in Quebec, 5% GST and 0% QST.
- KDP royalties are zero-rated only if the paying Amazon entity is not registered under the normal regime. Check which entity pays you.
- Most first books stay below the threshold, so no tax is charged and no input tax credits are claimed.

### Gaps
- BC PST on books.
- Which Amazon entity pays KDP royalties, and its GST/HST status.
- Revenu Québec primary pages.

## 8. US tax

### Takeaway
File a W-8BEN claiming treaty Article XII(3)(a), which gives 0% on literary and artistic copyright royalties; without it, 30% is withheld. No US return is needed if the correct tax is withheld.

### Cited Findings
- **W-8BEN:**
  - "Do not send Form W-8BEN to the IRS"; give it to the payer.
  - Put your foreign TIN on line 6a. Without it, an ITIN is generally required for treaty claims.
  - Line 10 is required "if the treaty contains different withholding rates for different types of royalties".
  - The form is valid until the end of the third calendar year after signing. Report changes within 30 days.
  - Instructions Rev. 10/2021, reviewed 2026-04-30. — [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben)
- **Treaty:** XII(2) caps royalties at 10%. Under XII(3)(a), "copyright royalties... in respect of the production or reproduction of any literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work" (film and TV excluded) are taxable only where the owner lives. XII(5) is the permanent-establishment exception. — [Finance Canada treaty text](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/tax-policy/tax-treaties/country/united-states-america-convention-consolidated-1980-1983-1984-1995-1997-2007.html)
- **KDP:** an online tax questionnaire, with no TIN needed to complete it. The default is the "statutory withholding tax rate (30%)". A foreign tax ID can be used to claim treaty benefits. — [KDP tax help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201723290)
- **IngramSpark:** no official withholding page found. Its user guide (2026-08-24) mentions uploading tax forms (unverified). — [IngramSpark guide](https://www.ingramspark.com/hubfs/downloads/user-guide.pdf)
- **Filing:** A nonresident with US-source income must file a 1040-NR if "not all of the U.S. tax that you owe was withheld". You "should file one if you can get money back". — [IRS 1040-NR (2025)](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040nr)

### Inferences
- On line 10, enter Article XII(3)(a), 0%, literary copyright royalties. Use your SIN on line 6a; this is common practice, but the IRS does not name the SIN.
- With 0% properly applied there is nothing to file in the US. Report all royalties to CRA.

### Gaps
- Whether Form 1042-S is issued.
- IngramSpark's withholding process.

## 9. CASL (email marketing)

### Takeaway
A newsletter needs express opt-in consent, or time-limited implied consent. Every message needs sender identification and an unsubscribe that is honoured within 10 business days. Keep proof of consent.

### Cited Findings
- **Basic requirements:** consent, identification and an unsubscribe.
  - Identification: name, mailing address, and phone, email or website, valid for 60 days.
  - Unsubscribes "actioned within 10 business days or less and at no cost".
  - Express consent never expires unless withdrawn. Implied consent lasts up to 2 years, or 6 months for inquiries.
  - Be ready to prove consent. — [ISED, 2019-04-01](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/node/7)
- **What counts as consent (s.10):**
  - A consent request must state its purpose and identify who is asking.
  - Implied consent exists only for:
    - an existing business or non-business relationship;
    - an address the person conspicuously published;
    - an address the person disclosed to you.
  - For the last two, the message must relate to the recipient's business or official role.
  - An existing business relationship means a purchase within 2 years, or an inquiry within 6 months. — [CASL s.10, current to 2026-10-29, amended 2026-01-01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/section-10.html)
- **Consent request content:** must include a statement "that the person whose consent is sought can withdraw their consent". Information must be "clearly and prominently" set out, and the unsubscribe "readily performed". — [SOR/2012-36](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-36/FullText.html)
- **Penalties:** maximum $1,000,000 for an individual and $10,000,000 for others. — [CASL s.20(4)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/section-20.html)

### Inferences
- Use active opt-in with a purpose line and log when and where consent was given.
- Amazon buyers give you no consent, because you never get their addresses. Direct buyers give 2 years; reader inquiries give 6 months.
- Published addresses of librarians or teachers can be used only for messages relevant to their role.

### Gaps
- CRTC guidance (blocked), including its position on pre-checked boxes.
- What the 2026-01-01 amendment changed.
- How CASL applies to non-Canadian recipients.

## 10. Children's product safety

### Takeaway
- **Canada:** books are consumer products. The lead regulations cover children's books but exempt conventional paper and cardboard ones. The Toys Regulations don't mention books.
- **US:** ordinary books are exempt from lead testing. Books for children 3 and under, books with play value, and books with attachments are not.

### Cited Findings
- **Canada:**
  - **CCPSA:**
    - Consumer products include packaging, and books are not excluded.
    - Sections 7 and 8 ban products that are a danger to health or safety.
    - Section 13: keep supplier and customer records for 6 years.
    - Section 14: report incidents within 2 days, with a written report within 10 days. — [CCPSA, current to 2026-10-29](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-1.68/FullText.html)
  - **Lead regulations:** They cover books for children under 14, except those "printed on paper or cardboard, and printed and bound in a conventional manner using conventional materials". They also cover play and learning products for under-14s and sleep and hygiene products for under-4s. Limit: 90 mg/kg of lead in accessible parts. — [SOR/2018-83](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-83/FullText.html)
  - **Toys Regulations:**
    - A toy is "intended for use by a child under 14 years of age in learning or play". There is no mention of books.
    - Toys for children under 3 may not have small parts (small-parts cylinder, 4.45 N).
    - Warnings must be bilingual.
    - Coatings are limited to 90 mg/kg lead. — [SOR/2011-17](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-17/FullText.html)
  - Health Canada's toy guide is silent on books. — [HC guide, 2026-01-30](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/reports-publications/industry-professionals/industry-guide-safety-requirements-children-toys-related-products-summary/guidance-document.html)
- **United States:**
  - **Lead and the ordinary-book exemption:**
    - Children's products must meet a 100 ppm lead limit, but ordinary books are exempt from testing (PL 112-28).
    - The exemption excludes books with "inherent play value", books "designed or intended for a child 3 years of age or younger", toys packaged with a book, and non-paper parts.
    - A Children's Product Certificate (CPC) that relies on the exemption must cite PL 112-28. — [CPSC FAQ](https://cpsc.gov/FAQ/Childrens-Books)
  - **Tracking labels:** products for children 12 and under need the manufacturer, the place and month/year of production, and batch information, on the product and its packaging. The manufacturer or importer of record is responsible. — [CPSC tracking](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Tracking-Label)
  - **Ingram:** ordinary books have been exempt from third-party testing and certification since Aug 1, 2011. Ingram prints a plant-and-date code on the last page. — [Ingram](https://ingramcontent.com/page/product-safety)

### Inferences
- An ordinary hardcover picture book needs no lead testing in either country. Keep print records anyway.
- Board books: exempt in Canada if conventional cardboard. In the US, those for ages 3 and under need third-party testing and a CPC.
- Books with toys, plush, sound modules, foam, vinyl or cloth fall in scope in both countries.
- Importing an offset print run into the US (for example, to Amazon FBA) may make you the importer of record, with labeling and certification duties.

### Gaps
- CPSC certificate eFiling, reportedly effective July 2026 (unverified). — [CPSC eFiling](https://www.cpsc.gov/eFiling)
- Amazon's own requirements for children's products.

## 11. Gotchas and open questions

### Gotchas
- **ISBNs:** 2-month account wait; AI-heavy books risk a cap; a KDP free ISBN is KDP-only and shows "Independently published"; one ISBN per format.
- **Legal deposit:** 7 days, every physical format, and BAnQ too in Quebec.
- **CIP:** none for self-publishers.
- **Illustrations:** no signed assignment means the illustrator keeps copyright; "work for hire" does nothing; moral rights need a waiver.
- **CIPO:** $63 online, not the $50 still quoted on blogs.
- **GST/HST:** royalties count toward $30,000; zero-rating depends on who pays you; the rebate excludes ebooks; GI-065 rates are outdated.
- **W-8BEN:** line 10 is mandatory; renew about every 3 years.
- **Income tax:** home office can't create a loss; writers can't elect nil inventory.
- **CASL:** implied consent expires.
- **Product safety:** board, bath, cloth or toy-attached formats change the rules.
