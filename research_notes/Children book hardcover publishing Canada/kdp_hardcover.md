# KDP Hardcover for a Children's Picture Book: Canadian Self-Publisher (state as of 2026-11-24)

**Dating.** I opened all kdp.amazon.com help pages on 2026-11-24. KDP help pages carry no "last updated" date, but every one showed "© 1996-2026" and an "October 2026" banner, so they reflect the live state in late 2026. Third-party sources carry their date in the link label. "Unverified" means I saw it only in a search snippet. The shared web-search budget ran out near the end, so a few items remain open; they are numbered OQ1–OQ13 in the final section.

## 1. Hardcover specs

### Takeaway
KDP hardcovers are case laminate only, with no dust jacket and no cloth. There are five portrait trims (the largest is 8.25x11), page counts run from 75 to 550, and the ink options are black (white or cream paper) or premium color (white only). There is no square or landscape hardcover, so a 32-page square or landscape picture book needs a new layout and at least 75 pages.

### Cited Findings
- Trims are 5.5x8.5, 6x9, 6.14x9.21, 7x10 and 8.25x11. All are portrait; none is square or landscape — [KDP-PrintOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180). KDP says there are "five available trim sizes" — [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B). Kindlepreneur lists the same five — [Kindlepreneur-2026-04-08](https://kindlepreneur.com/self-publish-hardcover-book-amazon/).
- **Every trim and ink allows 75–550 pages, so the 75-page minimum still applies** — [KDP-PrintOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180); [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B).
- Black ink prints on 50–61 lb (74–90 GSM) cream or white paper. Premium color prints on 60–71 lb (88–105 GSM) white paper. "Standard color ink is not available for hardcover," and there is no groundwood paper — [KDP-PrintOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180).
- The cover is printed on 80 lb (220 GSM) stock with a glossy or matte finish — [KDP-PrintOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180). The book has 2 mm board, a flat spine, and white or cream endsheets that match the interior paper. Books over 120 pages get a headband — [KDP-PrintElements](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GKZVNAAFYWVKZWL8).
- "Dust jackets, cloth bound, and other hardcover formats aren't available" — [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B).
- Interior bleed and margins — [KDP-TrimBleedMargins](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6):
  - Bleed is optional for the interior. It is 0.125" (3.2 mm) on the top, bottom and outside edges.
  - A page with bleed is the trim plus 0.125" in width and 0.25" in height, so 8.25x11 becomes 8.375x11.25.
  - If one page bleeds, the whole file must be set up with bleed, and bleed works only with PDF.
  - For 24–150 pages, the inside margin is 0.375". Outside, top and bottom margins are at least 0.25" without bleed or at least 0.375" with bleed.
  - There is no separate margin table for hardcover.
- Cover file — [KDP-HardcoverCover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTKFJPNQCBTMRV6):
  - One PDF, with a 0.51" (15 mm) wrap. Keep content at least 0.635" (16 mm) from the edge.
  - A 0.4" (10 mm) hinge on each side of the spine must stay clear of text and barcodes.
  - Your own barcode must be 2"x1.2" and at least 0.76" from the bottom. Images must be 300 DPI.
  - Size the file with the [cover calculator](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/cover-calculator). KDP gives no spine-width formula.
- KDP says "Books under 79 pages can't have spine text" on a page covering both paperback and hardcover — [KDP-FormattingIssues](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834260). The paperback page instead says "more than 79 pages" — [KDP-PaperbackGuidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950).
- Blank pages are capped at 4 in a row at the start or middle and 10 at the end. Images must be at least 300 DPI and text at least 7 pt — [KDP-FormattingIssues](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834260).
- Hardcover covers have "extra wide margins," so a paperback cover file must be reworked — [Kindlepreneur-2026-04-08](https://kindlepreneur.com/self-publish-hardcover-book-amazon/).

### Inferences
- The only picture-book-shaped trims are 8.25x11 and 7x10. Both are "large" trims, which cost more (§3).
- A 32-page story has to reach at least 75 pages (76 if the count must be even, which I'm assuming). Because blank pages are capped, the extra pages need real content: activities, bonus spreads or back matter.
- A 76-page book gets no spine text, so aim for 80 or more pages.

## 2. Marketplaces, printing location, Expanded Distribution

### Takeaway
The primary hardcover marketplaces are Amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .es, .fr, .it, .nl, .ie, .com.be, .pl and .se. **Amazon.ca sells KDP hardcovers only when Amazon.com is enabled.** KDP's own pages disagree about Amazon.com.au. There is no Expanded Distribution and no Japan.

### Cited Findings
- KDP's hardcover page — [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B):
  - It gives the primary list above.
  - "Amazon.ca and Amazon.com.au aren't listed as primary marketplaces. To make your hardcover book available in those marketplaces, you'll need to make it available on Amazon.com."
  - "Hardcover isn't available for Amazon.co.jp, or Expanded Distribution."
  - Hardcover ads run on Amazon.ca and Amazon.com.au.
- KDP's distribution-rights page — [KDP-DistributionRights](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834280):
  - It lists "Amazon.ca*" for hardcover, with the same Amazon.com condition.
  - It shows Amazon.com.au for paperback only. This conflicts with the hardcover page.
  - "Expanded Distribution is not available for hardcover books."
  - A book made available in the US, Canada or Mexico is "eligible for sale in all countries."
- KDP doesn't say where hardcovers are printed — [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B). A c.2020 article says Canadian orders are printed and shipped from the US (unverified; [NewPublishingStandard](https://thenewpublishingstandard.com/?p=6287)).
- Kindlepreneur notes "no expanded distribution option" and calls IngramSpark the better choice for wide hardcover distribution — [Kindlepreneur-2026-04-08](https://kindlepreneur.com/self-publish-hardcover-book-amazon/).

### Inferences
- Amazon.ca is a secondary storefront for the hardcover, probably supplied through Amazon.com. That would fit with proofs being orderable only from Amazon.com (§4).
- KDP gives no route into Canadian bookstores or libraries.

- See OQ2 and OQ3.

## 3. Costs, royalties, minimum prices

### Takeaway
On Amazon.com, a premium-color hardcover in a large trim such as 8.25x11 costs USD 5.65 plus USD 0.080 per page to print. The royalty is 60% at USD 9.99 or more (CAD 13.99 or more on Amazon.ca) and 50% below that. The 50% tier introduced in June 2025 covers hardcovers, but it never applies in practice because every hardcover's minimum price is above USD 9.99. KDP publishes no CAD cost table for hardcovers.

### Cited Findings
- The formula is "Fixed cost + (page count * per page cost) = printing cost" — [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B).
- Amazon.com printing costs in USD — [KDP-HardcoverCost](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H):

  | Ink and pages | Regular trim | Large trim |
  |---|---|---|
  | Black, 75–108 pages (fixed price only) | 6.80 | 7.49 |
  | Black, 110–550 pages | 5.65 + 0.012/page | 5.65 + 0.017/page |
  | Premium color, 75–550 pages | 5.65 + 0.065/page | 5.65 + 0.080/page |

  - "Large" means wider than 6.12" or taller than 9".
  - Premium color, large trim, elsewhere: GBP 4.15 + 0.060 per page in the UK and EUR 4.65 + 0.072 in the EU.
  - **There is no table for Amazon.ca or Amazon.com.au.**
- Royalty rules — [KDP-HardcoverRoyalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS):
  - Printing cost depends on the marketplace where the book is ordered. Bleed and cover finish don't change it.
  - Royalty = (royalty rate × list price) − printing cost.
  - The 50% tier runs up to USD 9.98, CAD 13.98, AUD 13.98, GBP 7.98 and EUR 9.98. The 60% tier starts at USD 9.99, CAD 13.99, AUD 13.99, GBP 7.99 and EUR 9.99.
  - Maximum prices are USD 250, CAD 350, GBP 250 and EUR 250.
  - Some marketplaces add VAT or GST to the list price.
  - "KDP now offers both a 50% and 60% royalty rate." The page gives no date.
- The minimum list price is the printing cost divided by 50% or 60%. The maximum print price on Amazon.com.au is AUD 350 — [KDP-PrintPricing](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G8BKPU9AGVZSF9QF).
- The 50% rate below USD 9.99 took effect June 10, 2025; this source discusses paperbacks — [Dale-2025-05-14](https://selfpub.substack.com/p/kdp-print-royalty-change-2025-what-authors-need-to-know). Reedsy says authors get "60% … for every paperback or hardcover sold at $9.99 or more" — [Reedsy-2025-06-10](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/kdp/amazon-self-publishing-royalties/).
- A price change takes 72 hours to 5 business days to apply. Until then, royalties use the old price — [KDP-Timelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620).

### Inferences (my arithmetic from Amazon.com USD figures)
- **Worked example: a 76-page premium-color 8.25x11 hardcover.**
  - Printing cost: 5.65 + 76 × 0.080 = **USD 11.73**.
  - Minimum list price: 11.73 ÷ 0.60 = **USD 19.55**.
  - Royalty per Amazon.com sale: **USD 0.26 at 19.99, 3.26 at 24.99 and 6.26 at 29.99**.
- At 80 pages (enough for spine text), printing costs USD 12.05. The minimum price is then USD 20.08, and the royalty at USD 24.99 is USD 2.94.
- The cheapest possible hardcover costs USD 6.80, giving a minimum price of USD 11.33. No hardcover can be priced below USD 9.99, so the 2025 cut has no effect on hardcovers sold on Amazon.com.
- On Amazon.ca, the minimum is roughly the CAD equivalent of the printing cost divided by 0.60. Check it in the [KDP-Calculator](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GSQF43YAMUPFTMSP).

## 4. Proof and author copies

### Takeaway
Canadians order hardcover proofs and author copies from **Amazon.com**, paying the USD printing cost plus shipping and taxes. Canadian import taxes may apply at the border.

### Cited Findings
- "Hardcover proof and author copies are not currently available for Amazon.ca. To have hardcover proof and author copies delivered to Canada, select Amazon.com." The quantity caps are 5 proofs and 999 author copies — [KDP-OrderCopies](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVEG4YA9G2T7N6DR).
- Proofs carry a "Not for Resale" watermark and a unique barcode, with no ISBN. Author copies cost the print price and can be resold. Neither earns royalties, and author copies don't include unpublished draft changes — [KDP-ProofAuthorCopies](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G7BBN68RYX5UMDZF).
- The print price excludes shipping and taxes, which Amazon.com adds at checkout. Copy orders get no Prime shipping and can't be expedited — [KDP-HolidayFAQ](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVJ42QHSM22EKM2J). KDP gives no shipping formula; you can see the total at checkout without placing the order — [KDP-CopyShipping](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GG6GRS7TKXVG6AGW).
- Canadian thresholds for courier shipments from the US — [CBSA-CUSMA](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/services/cusma-aceum/lvs-efv-eng.html) (modified 2025-11-25):
  - Up to CAD 40: no duty and no tax.
  - CAD 40–150: tax only.
  - Over CAD 150: duty and tax.
  - By mail, only shipments up to CAD 20 are free.
- The recipient is the importer of record and may owe import taxes and fees, which Amazon's carriers pay on your behalf — [Amazon-Customs](https://digprjsurvey.amazon.com/csad/help/node/G26L6NHEDGERVR8W) (Amazon help mirror). Amazon may collect an "Import Fees Deposit" at checkout (unverified).

### Inferences
- In the worked example, each copy costs USD 11.73 plus shipping. An order of several copies will likely pass CAD 40 and attract GST/HST. By courier, no duty applies up to CAD 150.

## 5. Tax interview and payments (Canada)

### Takeaway
The W-8 tax profile is mandatory, and the default US withholding is 30%. The Canada–US treaty makes royalties on literary works taxable **only in Canada**, so expect 0% US withholding, but confirm the rate the tax interview shows. Canadian bank accounts receive **EFT in CAD** from every marketplace, with no minimum, about 60 days after month-end.

### Cited Findings
- KDP tax page — [KDP-TaxWithholding](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274690):
  - "All non-US Person must complete a tax profile to be eligible to publish books on KDP."
  - Ebook sales on Amazon.com, and print sales on Amazon.com, .co.jp, .com.au and .ca, face 30% withholding unless a treaty reduces it. The rate appears on the "W-8 form review page."
  - Give a US TIN if you have one. Otherwise give "the income tax identification number issued to you by the tax authority in your country of residence."
  - The W-8 expires on the last day of the third calendar year after signing. After that, withholding reverts to 30%.
- Treaty Article XII(3)(a): "Copyright royalties and other like payments in respect of the production or reproduction of any literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work" (film and TV are excluded) "shall be taxable only in that other State," which means the recipient's country of residence — [TaxConventionAct](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-10.7/page-5.html).
- IRS instructions for Form W-8BEN (revised 10/2021) — [IRS-W8BEN-Instr](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben):
  - Treaty claimants generally give either a foreign tax ID (line 6) or a US ITIN (line 5).
  - The line 6a foreign tax ID is mandatory for certain account holders, unless their country is on the IRS list of countries that don't issue one.
  - Line 10 is for claims with special conditions, such as royalties taxed at different treaty rates, and must cite the treaty article.
- Third-party guides list Canada at 0% for royalties (unverified; [Wise](https://wise.com/ca/blog/form-w-8ben-canada), [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11572)).
- For a Canadian bank account, every marketplace pays by direct deposit in CAD. Cheque and wire are listed as "N/A" — [KDP-PaymentOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJD7DFFX6X88AK46).
- Payment arrives 60 days after the end of the month in which royalties reach the threshold. "EFT Payments do not have a threshold." Each marketplace pays separately — [KDP-PaymentThresholds](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201207800).

### Inferences
- Enter your SIN as the foreign tax ID; no ITIN is needed.
- Expect 0% under Article XII(3)(a). The royalties are still taxable in Canada.
- Royalties in other currencies are converted to CAD. KDP doesn't document the rate.

### Gaps
- See OQ5.

## 6. Content policies (children's books)

### Takeaway
You must disclose AI-generated text and images, illustrations included. Since Sept 21, 2026, KDP allows only **two new titles per format per week**. Children's books are not low-content. Review takes about 72 hours, but **an Amazon.ca detail page can take up to 30 days**. Look Inside is turned on automatically and shows 20% of a print book.

### Cited Findings
- AI disclosure — [KDP-ContentGuidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390):
  - "We require you to inform us of AI-generated content (text, images, or translations)."
  - "AI-generated images include cover and interior images and artwork."
  - Heavy editing doesn't change AI-generated status. AI-assisted work doesn't need disclosure.
  - The page says nothing about showing the disclosure to customers, or about title caps.
- Weekly title cap:
  - "Two titles per format per week" from Monday, Sept 21 (2026). The cap "resets every Sunday at 12:00 AM UTC," applies to new titles only, and replaces a previous cap of 10 — [ALLi-2026-09-24](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/amazon-cuts-kdp-weekly-upload-limit/).
  - Hardbacks are included, and there are no backlist exceptions — [ALLi-podcast-2026-10-02](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/podcast-amazon-cuts-kdp-uploads-to-two-titles-a-week/).
  - Jane Friedman's headline confirms the cap and notes that caps began in fall 2023 — [JaneFriedman-2026-09-23](https://janefriedman.com/amazon-kdp-limits-authors-to-two-titles-per-format-per-week/) (paywalled).
  - A separate submission cap started Oct 5, 2026. Drafts and new editions count toward the caps; updates don't — [eBookPbook-2026-10-09](https://ebookpbook.com/2026/10/09/kdp-weekly-title-limits).
  - A 2023 cap of 3 titles per day is unverified.
- Low-content books — [KDP-LowContent](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM):
  - Low-content means "minimal or no content." "Children's books are generally not low-content," and activity books are typically excluded too.
  - A low-content title must tick the low-content box, or it is rejected.
  - Low-content books get no free KDP ISBN and no Expanded Distribution. Without an ISBN, they get no Look Inside. Hardcover is allowed.
  - Reviews of low-content and activity books "might take 3-4 weeks."
  - Books created after November 2025 get no Transparency codes.
- Timelines — [KDP-Timelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620):
  - A book goes live in about 3 business days.
  - A print detail page appears within 72 hours on Amazon.com and within 5 days elsewhere, but can take "up to 30 days" on Amazon.ca.
  - A first book can take up to 6 days. The back-cover image appears in 7–10 business days.
  - Title, subtitle and author can be changed only within 72 hours of first publication. After that, you need a new edition.
  - For a set release date, the final file is due at least 5 days before.
- KDP's list of common rejection causes — [KDP-FormattingIssues](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834260):
  - Wrong trim or bleed.
  - Content outside the margins or in the gutter, including italics and hidden characters.
  - Images under 300 DPI, or enlarged after insertion.
  - Interior details that don't match the title, author, ISBN or imprint you entered.
  - Fonts that aren't embedded.
  - Too many blank pages, pagination errors, or leftover template text.
  - Text under 7 pt, or text that blends into the background.
  - Spine text on a book under 79 pages.
  - A wrong cover size, or crop marks.
  - Unflattened transparencies, and moiré patterns.
- Look Inside — [KDP-ReadSample](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644250):
  - It is automatic. It shows 20% of a print book (10% of an ebook), 9–10 business days after the book goes on sale, and needs an ISBN.
  - Print samples aren't supported on Amazon.com.au, .es, .it, .mx, .br or .nl. Amazon.ca isn't on that list.
  - KDP support can remove it.

### Inferences
- AI illustrations must be disclosed even after retouching.
- The ebook, paperback and hardcover of one book each use one weekly slot.
- Padding with activity pages might trigger the slower activity-book review (speculative).
- 20% of 76 pages is about 15 pages of preview.

### Gaps
- See OQ7.

## 7. Metadata

### Takeaway
A title gets 3 categories and up to 7 keywords. Reading age uses bands (picture books are 3–5), and KDP documents no grade-level field. Hardcovers support series and automatic linking with other formats. A+ Content works on Amazon.ca. The Amazon.ca Author Page is a copy of the US data and can't be edited.

### Cited Findings
- KDP says "you can select 3 categories." Children's categories need a reading age of 0–2, 3–5, 6–8 or 9–12. Answering "Yes" to sexually explicit content bars them — [KDP-Categories](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652170).
- KDP allows "up to seven keywords or short phrases," set per format. It bans other authors' names, brands you don't own, Amazon program names such as "Kindle+," quotation marks, and time-sensitive words, and has "zero tolerance" for misleading metadata — [KDP-Keywords](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201298500).
- Reading age — [KDP-PrimaryAudience](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201506310):
  - It is optional, but leaving it blank may keep the book out of Children's categories and age-based search.
  - Bands include 0–2 "Simple picture books with few or no words" and 3–5 "Picture books."
  - A customer-recommended age overrides yours.
  - The page mentions no grade-level field, although KDP's older children's page still mentions "Age and Grade tools" — [KDP-KidsPage](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/how-to-publish-childrens-books).
- A series can start with a hardcover, via "Create series page" or "Add to series." Link the formats first. The series page appears within 72 hours. Low-content books, public-domain books and print boxsets are excluded — [KDP-Series](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201757800).
- Formats link automatically "if your book details match" — [KDP-LinkBooks](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652220). Linking takes 48 hours to a week and needs the metadata to "exactly match" — [KDP-Timelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620). Which fields are matched, and the fallback of sending support the ASIN and ISBN, are unverified.
- Each format needs its own ISBN. KDP's free ISBN (paperback and hardcover) shows the imprint "Independently published" — [KDP-ISBN](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834170).
- A+ Content — [KDP-APlus](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHL7P99B7AA543CN):
  - It works for live titles and pre-orders.
  - From kdp.amazon.com it can be published to **Amazon.ca**, .com, .com.au, .com.br, .com.mx, .co.uk, .de, .es, .fr, .in, .it and .nl.
  - One project can cover several ASINs, but it must be published in each marketplace separately.
  - Content appears within 8 business days. No fee is mentioned.
- Author Central — [KDP-AuthorCentral](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644310):
  - Full Author Central covers Amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .fr, .co.jp and .com.br.
  - On Amazon.ca, Author Pages "will be created if you have books available." The content is "collected from the US marketplace and cannot currently be edited."
  - Set it up from the KDP Marketing page.

### Inferences
- Keep the title, author and series identical across formats so they link.
- Maintain the Amazon.com Author Page, since Amazon.ca copies it.
- Use the 3–5 reading age.

### Gaps
- See OQ9. KDP also doesn't say whether category choices are stored per format.

## 8. Ebook companion

### Takeaway
Kindle Kids' Book Creator is retired: downloads ended Feb 6, 2025, and MOBI files have been refused since Mar 18, 2025. Use Kindle Create (KPF files) or a fixed-layout EPUB, which is the format to use for text pop-ups.

### Cited Findings
- KDP's announcement of Jan 21, 2025 said Kindle Kids' Book Creator would be "unavailable for download from February 6, 2025" and "we will not accept Mobi files for publication after March 18, 2025." It recommended KPF (via Kindle Create) or EPUB — [MobileRead-2025-01-21](https://www.mobileread.com/forums/showthread.php?p=4483280).
- "For fixed-layout eBooks, use KPF (Kindle Create) files or EPUB." Fixed-layout EPUBs with text pop-ups are still accepted. Guided View doesn't show in the Online Previewer — [KDP-MOBI-FAQ](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GULSQMHU5MNH4EZM).
- KDP's children's page still offers the Kids' Book Creator, so it is out of date — [KDP-KidsPage](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/how-to-publish-childrens-books).
- "Effective July 7, 2026, the 70% royalty option price band is $2.99-$12.99 on Amazon.com." On Amazon.ca the band is CAD 2.99–12.99. Delivery costs still apply — [KDP-eBookRoyalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560).
- KDP pages now refer to "Kindle+ or Kindle Owner Lending Library" — [KDP-Timelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620). Reedsy says Kindle Unlimited was renamed Kindle+ "prior to October 2026" — [Reedsy-2025-06-10](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/kdp/amazon-self-publishing-royalties/).
- It is unverified that Kindle Create imports a PDF or one JPG per page, makes text hard to edit afterwards, and produces Amazon-only output — [ApexAuthors](https://apexauthors.com/kindle-create/) (I saw only a snippet; the page returned an error).

### Inferences
- Image-heavy files raise delivery costs under the 70% royalty.

## 9. Changes in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
I found no new hardcover trims, page limits or marketplaces. The changes were to royalties, tools, barcodes and upload caps.

### Cited Findings
- **Feb 6 and Mar 18, 2025:** the Kids' Book Creator and MOBI were retired — [MobileRead-2025-01-21](https://www.mobileread.com/forums/showthread.php?p=4483280).
- **Jun 10, 2025:** a 50% print tier was added, covering hardcovers — [Dale-2025-05-14](https://selfpub.substack.com/p/kdp-print-royalty-change-2025-what-authors-need-to-know); [KDP-HardcoverRoyalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS).
- **Nov 2025:** Transparency codes were retired — [KDP-LowContent](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM).
- **Jul 7, 2026:** the ebook 70% band rose to USD/CAD 12.99 — [KDP-eBookRoyalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560).

## 10. Setup sequence for a hardcover

### Takeaway
The order is: account, details, content, rights and pricing, proof, publish, then post-launch tasks.

### Cited Findings
1. **Account:** complete the tax profile — [KDP-TaxWithholding](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274690) — and add a bank account for CAD EFT — [KDP-PaymentOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJD7DFFX6X88AK46).
2. **Details:**
   - Use the same title, author and series as your other formats; they lock 72 hours after publication — [KDP-Timelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620).
   - Set the Primary Audience — [KDP-PrimaryAudience](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201506310).
   - Add up to 7 keywords — [KDP-Keywords](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201298500) — and choose 3 categories — [KDP-Categories](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652170).
3. **Content:**
   - Assign a hardcover ISBN — [KDP-ISBN](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834170).
   - Choose ink and paper, trim, bleed and finish — [KDP-PrintOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180).
   - Upload the interior PDF and the hardcover cover PDF — [KDP-HardcoverCover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTKFJPNQCBTMRV6).
   - Answer the AI disclosure — [KDP-ContentGuidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390).
   - Order a proof from Amazon.com — [KDP-OrderCopies](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVEG4YA9G2T7N6DR).
4. **Rights and pricing:**
   - Choose territories — [KDP-DistributionRights](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834280) — and enable Amazon.com, which Amazon.ca requires.
   - KDP lets you set "separate prices and distribution options for each supported marketplace" — [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B). The Amazon.ca maximum is CAD 350 — [KDP-HardcoverRoyalty](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS).
5. **Post-launch:** add A+ Content in each marketplace — [KDP-APlus](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHL7P99B7AA543CN) — and set up the Author Page — [KDP-AuthorCentral](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644310).

### Inferences
- Create all three formats in the same week, using one slot per format.

## Gotchas
- **The 75-page minimum, with blank padding capped** — [KDP-FormattingIssues](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834260).
- **No square or landscape hardcover, premium color only, and large-trim cost of USD 5.65 + 0.080 per page** — [KDP-HardcoverCost](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H).
- **No spine text under 79 pages** — [KDP-FormattingIssues](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834260).
- **The hardcover cover template differs from the paperback one** (0.51" wrap, 0.4" hinge) — [KDP-HardcoverCover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTKFJPNQCBTMRV6).
- **Amazon.ca needs Amazon.com enabled.** There is no CAD cost table, and the Amazon.ca page can take up to 30 days to appear — [KDP-DistributionRights](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834280); [KDP-Timelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620).
- **Hardcover proofs and author copies come only from Amazon.com.** You pay USD plus shipping, and possibly GST/HST above CAD 40 — [KDP-OrderCopies](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVEG4YA9G2T7N6DR); [CBSA-CUSMA](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/services/cusma-aceum/lvs-efv-eng.html).
- **No Expanded Distribution, dust jacket or cloth** — [KDP-Hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B).
- **The royalty is thin near the minimum price:** USD 0.26 at USD 19.99 in the worked example (§3).
- **The weekly cap is 2 titles per format, and drafts and new editions count** — [eBookPbook-2026-10-09](https://ebookpbook.com/2026/10/09/kdp-weekly-title-limits).
- **AI-generated illustrations, including the cover, must be disclosed** — [KDP-ContentGuidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390).
- **KDP's own children's page is out of date** — [KDP-KidsPage](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/how-to-publish-childrens-books).
- **The Amazon.ca Author Page can't be edited** — [KDP-AuthorCentral](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644310).
- **If the W-8 lapses, withholding reverts to 30%** — [KDP-TaxWithholding](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274690).
- **Canadian bank accounts get CAD EFT only, paid separately for each marketplace** — [KDP-PaymentOptions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJD7DFFX6X88AK46).
- **A blank reading age can keep the book out of Children's categories** — [KDP-PrimaryAudience](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201506310).
- **Each format needs its own ISBN, and KDP's free ISBN shows the imprint "Independently published"** — [KDP-ISBN](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834170).
